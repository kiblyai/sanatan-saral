Chapter 1 · 47 verses

Arjuna's Despair

Every verse of chapter 1, अर्जुनविषादयोगः, with the Sanskrit, its pronunciation and a simple meaning in English and Hindi. Verses marked with a link are explained in depth on the main Gita page.

Verses 32–46 are Arjuna's arguments, spoken in grief and confusion: his fears about killing family, the collapse of family traditions and the social order. They are not Krishna's teaching. Krishna answers them from chapter 2 onward, and commentators read these verses as a picture of a mind overwhelmed by attachment.

1.1 · Dhritarashtra

Gita 1.1

धर्मक्षेत्रे कुरुक्षेत्रे समवेता युयुत्सवः ।
मामकाः पाण्डवाश्चैव किमकुर्वत सञ्जय ॥

dharmakṣetre kurukṣetre samavetā yuyutsavaḥ | māmakāḥ pāṇḍavāścaiva kimakurvata sañjaya ||

Word by word
धर्म-क्षेत्रे
in the field of dharma
कुरु-क्षेत्रे
at Kurukshetra
समवेताः
gathered
युयुत्सवः
eager to fight
मामकाः
my sons
पाण्डवाः च एव
and the sons of Pandu
किम् अकुर्वत
what did they do?
सञ्जय
O Sanjaya
Translation

Dhritarashtra said: Sanjaya, on the holy field of Kurukshetra, gathered and eager to fight, what did my sons and the sons of Pandu do?

Read the full explanation →

📖 A story for this verse

From the Mahabharata (Bhishma Parva)

Before the great war began, the sage Vyasa came to the blind king Dhritarashtra and offered him sight so he could watch the battle. The king refused. "I do not want to see my own family being killed," he said, "but I do want to hear everything."

So Vyasa gave the gift of divine sight to Sanjaya, the king's charioteer. Sitting in the palace, far from the field, Sanjaya could see every corner of Kurukshetra, even the thoughts in the warriors' minds.

The whole Gita is Sanjaya's report to the anxious king. It opens with this verse: a father's worried question, "What did my sons do?" The answer he gets is far bigger than a war report.

पतन्ति पितरो ह्येषां लुप्तपिण्डोदकक्रियाः ॥

saṅkaro narakāyaiva kulaghnānāṃ kulasya ca | patanti pitaro hyeṣāṃ luptapiṇḍodakakriyāḥ ||

Word by word
सङ्करः
this confusion
नरकाय एव
leads only to hell
कुल-घ्नानां कुलस्य च
for the family's destroyers and the family
पतन्ति पितरः हि एषां
their ancestors fall
लुप्त-पिण्ड-उदक-क्रियाः
deprived of the offerings of food and water
Translation

This confusion drags the family and its destroyers to hell, and their ancestors fall, deprived of the offerings of food and water.

Gita 1.43

दोषैरेतैः कुलघ्नानां वर्णसङ्करकारकैः ।
उत्साद्यन्ते जातिधर्माः कुलधर्माश्च शाश्वताः ॥

doṣairetaiḥ kulaghnānāṃ varṇasaṅkarakārakaiḥ | utsādyante jātidharmāḥ kuladharmāśca śāśvatāḥ ||

Word by word
दोषैः एतैः
by these wrongs
कुल-घ्नानां
of the destroyers of the family
वर्ण-सङ्कर-कारकैः
which cause social confusion
उत्साद्यन्ते
are destroyed
जाति-धर्माः
the duties of the community
कुल-धर्माः च शाश्वताः
and the lasting family traditions
Translation

Through these wrongs of the family-destroyers, the lasting duties of community and family are ruined.

Gita 1.44

उत्सन्नकुलधर्माणां मनुष्याणां जनार्दन ।
नरकेऽनियतं वासो भवतीत्यनुशुश्रुम ॥

utsannakuladharmāṇāṃ manuṣyāṇāṃ janārdana | narake'niyataṃ vāso bhavatītyanuśuśruma ||

Word by word
उत्सन्न-कुल-धर्माणां
whose family traditions are destroyed
मनुष्याणां
of such people
जनार्दन
O Janardana
नरके अनियतं वासः
dwelling in hell without end
भवति
happens
इति अनुशुश्रुम
so we have heard
Translation

We have heard, Janardana, that people whose family duties are destroyed live in hell for a long time.

Gita 1.45

अहो बत महत्पापं कर्तुं व्यवसिता वयम् ।
यद्राज्यसुखलोभेन हन्तुं स्वजनमुद्यताः ॥

aho bata mahatpāpaṃ kartuṃ vyavasitā vayam | yadrājyasukhalobhena hantuṃ svajanamudyatāḥ ||

Word by word
अहो बत
alas!
महत् पापं
a great sin
कर्तुं व्यवसिताः वयम्
we are set on committing
यत्
that
राज्य-सुख-लोभेन
out of greed for kingdom and pleasure
हन्तुं स्वजनम् उद्यताः
we are ready to kill our own people
Translation

Alas! What a great sin we are set on doing, ready to kill our own people out of greed for the pleasures of a kingdom!

Gita 1.46

यदि मामप्रतीकारमशस्त्रं शस्त्रपाणयः ।
धार्तराष्ट्रा रणे हन्युस्तन्मे क्षेमतरं भवेत् ॥

yadi māmapratīkāramaśastraṃ śastrapāṇayaḥ | dhārtarāṣṭrā raṇe hanyustanme kṣemataraṃ bhavet ||

Word by word
यदि माम्
if me
अप्रतीकारम्
not resisting
अशस्त्रं
unarmed
शस्त्र-पाणयः
weapons in hand
धार्तराष्ट्राः
the sons of Dhritarashtra
रणे हन्युः
were to kill in battle
तत् मे क्षेमतरं भवेत्
that would be better for me
Translation

It would be better for me if the sons of Dhritarashtra, weapons in hand, killed me in battle while I stood unarmed and did not fight back."

Gita 1.47

एवमुक्त्वाऽर्जुनः संख्ये रथोपस्थ उपाविशत् ।
विसृज्य सशरं चापं शोकसंविग्नमानसः ॥

evamuktvā'rjunaḥ saṃkhye rathopastha upāviśat | visṛjya saśaraṃ cāpaṃ śokasaṃvignamānasaḥ ||

Word by word
एवम् उक्त्वा
having said this
अर्जुनः
Arjuna
संख्ये
on the battlefield
रथ-उपस्थे उपाविशत्
sat down on the seat of the chariot
विसृज्य
casting aside
स-शरं चापं
his bow and arrows
शोक-संविग्न-मानसः
his mind overwhelmed by grief
Translation

Sanjaya said: Having said this on the battlefield, Arjuna threw down his bow and arrows and sank down on the seat of the chariot, his mind overwhelmed with grief.
