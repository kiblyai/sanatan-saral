18.1 · Arjuna

Gita 18.1

अर्जुन उवाच
संन्यासस्य महाबाहो तत्त्वमिच्छामि वेदितुम् ।
त्यागस्य च हृषीकेश पृथक्केशिनिषूदन ॥

saṃnyāsasya mahābāho tattvamicchāmi veditum | tyāgasya ca hṛṣīkeśa pṛthakkeśiniṣūdana ||

Word by word
संन्यासस्य तत्त्वम् इच्छामि वेदितुम्
I wish to know the truth about renunciation
महा-बाहो
O mighty-armed (Krishna)
त्यागस्य च पृथक्
and, separately, about letting go
हृषीकेश
O Hrishikesha (Krishna)
केशि-निषूदन
O slayer of Keshi (Krishna)
Translation

Arjuna said: Krishna, I wish to know the truth about renunciation, and also about letting go, and how the two differ.

18.2 · Krishna

Gita 18.2

श्री भगवानुवाच
काम्यानां कर्मणां न्यासं संन्यासं कवयो विदुः ।
सर्वकर्मफलत्यागं प्राहुस्त्यागं विचक्षणाः ॥

kāmyānāṃ karmaṇāṃ nyāsaṃ saṃnyāsaṃ kavayo viduḥ | sarvakarmaphalatyāgaṃ prāhustyāgaṃ vicakṣaṇāḥ ||

Word by word
काम्यानां कर्मणां न्यासम्
giving up actions done out of desire
संन्यासं कवयः विदुः
the sages know as renunciation
सर्व-कर्म-फल-त्यागम्
giving up the results of all actions
प्राहुः त्यागं विचक्षणाः
the wise call letting go
Translation

Krishna said: The sages understand renunciation as giving up actions that are done out of desire. The wise call the giving up of the results of all actions letting go.

18.3 · Krishna

Gita 18.3

त्याज्यं दोषवदित्येके कर्म प्राहुर्मनीषिणः ।
यज्ञदानतपःकर्म न त्याज्यमिति चापरे ॥

tyājyaṃ doṣavadityeke karma prāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ | yajñadānatapaḥkarma na tyājyamiti cāpare ||

Word by word
त्याज्यं दोषवत् इति एके कर्म प्राहुः मनीषिणः

यज्ञ-दान-तपः-कर्म न त्याज्यम् इति च अपरे

Translation

Some thinkers say that all action is flawed and should be given up. Others say that acts of sacrifice, giving and self-discipline should not be given up.

Gita 18.6

But even these actions should be done without attachment and without a claim on their results. This, Arjuna, is my firm and final view.

Gita 18.8

duḥkhamityeva yatkarma kāyakleśabhayāttyajet | sa kṛtvā rājasaṃ tyāgaṃ naiva tyāgaphalaṃ labhet ||

Whoever gives up a duty just because it is painful, or out of fear of bodily trouble, lets go in the way of passion and gains none of the reward of letting go.
