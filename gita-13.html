Chapter 13 · 34 verses

The Field and the Knower of the Field

Every verse of chapter 13, क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञविभागयोगः, with the Sanskrit, its pronunciation, the meaning of each word and a translation in simple English. Verses marked with a link are explained in depth on the main Gita page.

Verses 1–6: the body is the field, and the one who knows it is the knower of the field; 7–11: twenty qualities that together are called knowledge; 12–18: Brahman, the thing to be known; 19–23: nature and the spirit; 24–26: different ways of seeing the Self; 27–34: the one Lord, the same in all beings, who does not act and is not stained. Some editions begin this chapter with a question from Arjuna and count 35 verses; the numbering here follows the 34-verse count.

13.1 · Krishna

Gita 13.1

श्री भगवानुवाच
इदं शरीरं कौन्तेय क्षेत्रमित्यभिधीयते ।
एतद्यो वेत्ति तं प्राहुः क्षेत्रज्ञ इति तद्विदः ॥

idaṃ śarīraṃ kaunteya kṣetramityabhidhīyate | etadyo vetti taṃ prāhuḥ kṣetrajña iti tadvidaḥ ||

Word by word
इदं शरीरम्
this body
कौन्तेय
O son of Kunti (Arjuna)
क्षेत्रम् इति अभिधीयते
is called the field
एतत् यः वेत्ति
the one who knows it
तं प्राहुः क्षेत्रज्ञः इति
is called the knower of the field
तत्-विदः
by those who understand these things
Translation

Krishna said: This body, Arjuna, is called the field. The one who knows it is called the knower of the field by those who understand these things.

13.2 · Krishna

Gita 13.2

क्षेत्रज्ञं चापि मां विद्धि सर्वक्षेत्रेषु भारत ।
क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञयोर्ज्ञानं यत्तज्ज्ञानं मतं मम ॥

kṣetrajñaṃ cāpi māṃ viddhi sarvakṣetreṣu bhārata | kṣetrakṣetrajñayorjñānaṃ yattajjñānaṃ mataṃ mama ||

Word by word
क्षेत्रज्ञं च अपि मां विद्धि
know me also as the knower of the field
सर्व-क्षेत्रेषु
in all fields
भारत
O descendant of Bharata (Arjuna)
क्षेत्र-क्षेत्रज्ञयोः ज्ञानम्
knowledge of the field and its knower
यत् तत् ज्ञानं मतं मम
that, in my view, is true knowledge
Translation

Know me, Arjuna, as the knower of the field in every field. Knowledge of the field and of its knower is, in my view, true knowledge.

13.3 · Krishna

Gita 13.3

तत्क्षेत्रं यच्च यादृक् च यद्विकारि यतश्च यत् ।
स च यो यत्प्रभावश्च तत्समासेन मे शृणु ॥

tatkṣetraṃ yacca yādṛk ca yadvikāri yataśca yat | sa ca yo yatprabhāvaśca tatsamāsena me śṛṇu ||

Word by word
तत् क्षेत्रं यत् च
what that field is
यादृक् च
and what it is like
यत्-विकारि
what its changes are
यतः च यत्
and where it comes from
सः च यः
and who he, the knower, is
यत्-प्रभावः च
and what his powers are
तत् समासेन मे शृणु
hear that from me in brief
Translation

Hear from me in brief what the field is, what it is like, how it changes and where it comes from, and also who the knower is and what his powers are.

Gita 13.12
