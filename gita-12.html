Chapter 12 · 20 verses

The Yoga of Devotion

Every verse of chapter 12, भक्तियोगः, with the Sanskrit, its pronunciation, the meaning of each word and a translation in simple English. Verses marked with a link are explained in depth on the main Gita page.

Verses 1–7: is it better to worship Krishna in a form, or the formless? Both reach him, but the first way is easier; 8–12: four steps, from fixing the mind on him down to giving up the results of action; 13–20: the qualities of the devotee who is dear to Krishna.

12.1 · Arjuna

Gita 12.1

अर्जुन उवाच
एवं सततयुक्ता ये भक्तास्त्वां पर्युपासते ।
ये चाप्यक्षरमव्यक्तं तेषां के योगवित्तमाः ॥

evaṃ satatayuktā ye bhaktāstvāṃ paryupāsate | ye cāpyakṣaramavyaktaṃ teṣāṃ ke yogavittamāḥ ||

Word by word
एवं सतत-युक्ताः ये भक्ताः
the devotees who are always united with you in this way
त्वां पर्युपासते
and worship you
ये च अपि अक्षरम् अव्यक्तम्
and those who worship the imperishable, the unseen
तेषां के योग-वित्तमाः
which of them know yoga best
Translation

Arjuna asked: Some devotees worship you, always united with you in this way. Others worship the imperishable and unseen. Which of them know yoga best?

12.2 · Krishna

Gita 12.2

श्री भगवानुवाच
मय्यावेश्य मनो ये मां नित्ययुक्ता उपासते ।
श्रद्धया परयोपेतास्ते मे युक्ततमा मताः ॥

mayyāveśya mano ye māṃ nityayuktā upāsate | śraddhayā parayopetāste me yuktatamā matāḥ ||

Word by word
मयि आवेश्य मनः
fixing their minds on me
ये मां नित्य-युक्ताः उपासते
those who worship me, always united with me
श्रद्धया परया उपेताः
full of the highest faith
ते मे युक्ततमाः मताः
I consider them the best in yoga
Translation

Krishna said: Those who fix their minds on me and worship me, always united with me and full of the highest faith, I consider the best in yoga.

12.5 · Krishna

Gita 12.5

क्लेशोऽधिकतरस्तेषामव्यक्तासक्तचेतसाम् ।
अव्यक्ता हि गतिर्दुःखं देहवद्भिरवाप्यते ॥

kleśo'dhikatarasteṣāmavyaktāsaktacetasām | avyaktā hi gatirduḥkhaṃ dehavadbhiravāpyate ||

Word by word
क्लेशः अधिकतरः तेषाम्
the difficulty is greater for those
अव्यक्त-आसक्त-चेतसाम्
whose minds are set on the unseen
अव्यक्ता हि गतिः
for the path of the unseen
दुःखं देहवद्भिः अवाप्यते
is hard for those with a body to reach
Translation

The difficulty is greater for those whose minds are set on the unseen, for the path of the unseen is hard for people who live in a body.

12.6 · Krishna

Gita 12.6

ये तु सर्वाणि कर्माणि मयि संन्यस्य मत्पराः ।
अनन्येनैव योगेन मां ध्यायन्त उपासते ॥

ye tu sarvāṇi karmāṇi mayi saṃnyasya matparāḥ | ananyenaiva yogena māṃ dhyāyanta upāsate ||

Word by word
ये तु सर्वाणि कर्माणि मयि संन्यस्य
but those who give up all their actions to me
मत्-पराः
holding me as the highest
अनन्येन एव योगेन
with a yoga that has no other object
मां ध्यायन्तः उपासते

Translation

But those who offer all their actions to me, hold me as their highest goal, and worship me, meditating on me with undivided yoga,

Gita 12.8

12.12 · Krishna

Gita 12.12

श्रेयो हि ज्ञानमभ्यासाज्ज्ञानाद्ध्यानं विशिष्यते ।
ध्यानात्कर्मफलत्यागस्त्यागाच्छान्तिरनन्तरम् ॥

śreyo hi jñānamabhyāsājjñānāddhyānaṃ viśiṣyate | dhyānātkarmaphalatyāgastyāgācchāntiranantaram ||

Word by word
श्रेयः हि ज्ञानम् अभ्यासात्
knowledge is better than practice
ज्ञानात् ध्यानं विशिष्यते
meditation is better than knowledge
ध्यानात् कर्म-फल-त्यागः
giving up the results of action is better than meditation
त्यागात् शान्तिः अनन्तरम्
from giving up, peace follows at once
Translation

Knowledge is better than mere practice, and meditation is better than knowledge. Giving up the results of action is better than meditation, for peace follows at once from giving up.
