Chapter 16 · 24 verses

Divine and Demonic Qualities

Every verse of chapter 16, दैवासुरसम्पद्विभागयोगः, with the Sanskrit, its pronunciation, the meaning of each word and a translation in simple English. Verses marked with a link are explained in depth on the main Gita page.

Verses 1–3: twenty-six divine qualities; 4–5: the demonic qualities, and where each kind leads; 6–18: how demonic people think and act; 19–20: what becomes of them; 21–22: desire, anger and greed, the three gates to hell; 23–24: the scriptures as a guide to what should be done.

Gita 16.1

श्री भगवानुवाच
अभयं सत्त्वसंशुद्धिर्ज्ञानयोगव्यवस्थितिः ।

Gita 16.3

तेजः क्षमा धृतिः शौचमद्रोहो नातिमानिता ।
भवन्ति सम्पदं दैवीमभिजातस्य भारत ॥

tejaḥ kṣamā dhṛtiḥ śaucamadroho nātimānitā | bhavanti sampadaṃ daivīmabhijātasya bhārata ||

16.5 · Krishna

Gita 16.5

दैवी सम्पद्विमोक्षाय निबन्धायासुरी मता ।
मा शुचः सम्पदं दैवीमभिजातोऽसि पाण्डव ॥

daivī sampadvimokṣāya nibandhāyāsurī matā | mā śucaḥ sampadaṃ daivīmabhijāto'si pāṇḍava ||

Word by word
दैवी सम्पत् विमोक्षाय
the divine qualities lead to freedom
निबन्धाय आसुरी मता
the demonic are held to lead to bondage
मा शुचः
do not grieve
सम्पदं दैवीम् अभिजातः असि
you are born with the divine qualities
पाण्डव
O son of Pandu (Arjuna)
Translation

The divine qualities lead to freedom, and the demonic to bondage. Do not grieve, Arjuna: you are born with the divine qualities.

16.6 · Krishna

Gita 16.6

द्वौ भूतसर्गौ लोकेऽस्मिन् दैव आसुर एव च ।
दैवो विस्तरशः प्रोक्त आसुरं पार्थ मे शृणु ॥

dvau bhūtasargau loke'smin daiva āsura eva ca | daivo vistaraśaḥ prokta āsuraṃ pārtha me śṛṇu ||

Word by word
द्वौ भूत-सर्गौ लोके अस्मिन्
there are two kinds of beings in this world
दैवः आसुरः एव च
the divine and the demonic
दैवः विस्तरशः प्रोक्तः
the divine has been described at length
आसुरं मे शृणु
now hear from me about the demonic
पार्थ
O Partha (Arjuna)
Translation

There are two kinds of beings in this world, the divine and the demonic. I have described the divine at length. Now hear from me, Arjuna, about the demonic.

16.7 · Krishna

Gita 16.7

प्रवृत्तिं च निवृत्तिं च जना न विदुरासुराः ।
न शौचं नापि चाचारो न सत्यं तेषु विद्यते ॥

pravṛttiṃ ca nivṛttiṃ ca janā na vidurāsurāḥ | na śaucaṃ nāpi cācāro na satyaṃ teṣu vidyate ||

Word by word
प्रवृत्तिं च निवृत्तिं च
what to do and what not to do
जनाः न विदुः आसुराः
demonic people do not know
न शौचं न अपि च आचारः
neither purity nor good conduct
न सत्यं तेषु विद्यते
nor truth is found in them
Translation

Demonic people do not know what should be done and what should not be done. Neither purity, nor good conduct, nor truth is found in them.

Gita 16.8

अपरस्परसम्भूतं किमन्यत्कामहैतुकम् ॥

asatyamapratiṣṭhaṃ te jagadāhuranīśvaram | aparasparasambhūtaṃ kimanyatkāmahaitukam ||

Word by word
असत्यम् अप्रतिष्ठं ते जगत् आहुः
they say the world has no truth and no foundation
अनीश्वरम्
no Lord
अपरस्पर-सम्भूतम्

किम् अन्यत्

काम-हैतुकम्

They say that the world has no truth, no foundation and no Lord, that it is born only of the union of male and female, and that desire is its only cause.

Gita 16.22

एतैर्विमुक्तः कौन्तेय तमोद्वारैस्त्रिभिर्नरः ।
आचरत्यात्मनः श्रेयस्ततो याति परां गतिम् ॥

etairvimuktaḥ kaunteya tamodvāraistribhirnaraḥ | ācaratyātmanaḥ śreyastato yāti parāṃ gatim ||

Word by word
एतैः विमुक्तः
freed from these
कौन्तेय
O son of Kunti (Arjuna)
तमः-द्वारैः त्रिभिः नरः
a person, from the three gates of darkness
आचरति आत्मनः श्रेयः
does what is good for the soul
ततः याति परां गतिम्
and so reaches the highest goal
Translation

A person who is freed from these three gates of darkness, Arjuna, does what is good for the soul and so reaches the highest goal.

16.23 · Krishna

Gita 16.23

यः शास्त्रविधिमुत्सृज्य वर्तते कामकारतः ।
न स सिद्धिमवाप्नोति न सुखं न परां गतिम् ॥

yaḥ śāstravidhimutsṛjya vartate kāmakārataḥ | na sa siddhimavāpnoti na sukhaṃ na parāṃ gatim ||

Word by word
यः शास्त्र-विधिम् उत्सृज्य
whoever sets aside the guidance of the scriptures
वर्तते काम-कारतः
and acts as desire drives him
न सः सिद्धिम् अवाप्नोति
does not reach perfection
न सुखं न परां गतिम्
nor happiness, nor the highest goal
Translation

Whoever sets aside the guidance of the scriptures and acts as desire drives them reaches neither perfection, nor happiness, nor the highest goal.

Gita 16.24

तस्माच्छास्त्रं प्रमाणं ते कार्याकार्यव्यवस्थितौ ।
ज्ञात्वा शास्त्रविधानोक्तं कर्म कर्तुमिहार्हसि ॥

tasmācchāstraṃ pramāṇaṃ te kāryākāryavyavasthitau | jñātvā śāstravidhānoktaṃ karma kartumihārhasi ||

Word by word
तस्मात् शास्त्रं प्रमाणं ते
so let the scriptures be your guide
कार्य-अकार्य-व्यवस्थितौ
in deciding what to do and what not to do
ज्ञात्वा शास्त्र-विधान-उक्तम्
knowing what the scriptures teach
कर्म कर्तुम् इह अर्हसि
you should do your work in this world
Translation

So let the scriptures be your guide in deciding what to do and what not to do. Knowing what the scriptures teach, do your work in this world.
