Chapter 3 · 43 verses

The Yoga of Action

Every verse of chapter 3, कर्मयोगः, with the Sanskrit, its pronunciation and a simple meaning in English and Hindi. Verses marked with a link are explained in depth on the main Gita page.

Verses 1–8: no one can avoid action; 9–16: work as yajna, the cycle of giving; 17–29: acting for the good of the world, as Krishna himself does; 30–35: offering action to God and following one's own nature; 36–43: desire and anger as the real enemy, and how to defeat them.

3.1 · Arjuna

Gita 3.1

ज्यायसी चेत्कर्मणस्ते मता बुद्धिर्जनार्दन ।
तत्किं कर्मणि घोरे मां नियोजयसि केशव ॥

jyāyasī cetkarmaṇaste matā buddhirjanārdana | tatkiṃ karmaṇi ghore māṃ niyojayasi keśava ||

Word by word
ज्यायसी चेत्
if (it is) superior
कर्मणः
to action
ते मता बुद्धिः
understanding, in your view
जनार्दन
O Krishna
तत् किं
then why
कर्मणि घोरे
in this terrible action
मां नियोजयसि
do you urge me
केशव
O Krishna
Translation

Arjuna said: Janardana, if you think knowledge is better than action, then why do you push me into this terrible action, Keshava?

3.2 · Arjuna

Gita 3.2

व्यामिश्रेणेव वाक्येन बुद्धिं मोहयसीव मे ।
तदेकं वद निश्चित्य येन श्रेयोऽहमाप्नुयाम् ॥

vyāmiśreṇeva vākyena buddhiṃ mohayasīva me | tadekaṃ vada niścitya yena śreyo'hamāpnuyām ||

Word by word
व्यामिश्रेण इव वाक्येन
with seemingly mixed words
बुद्धिं मोहयसि इव मे
you seem to confuse my mind
तत् एकं वद
tell me that one thing
निश्चित्य
for certain
येन श्रेयः अहम् आप्नुयाम्
by which I may reach the highest good
Translation

Your words seem mixed and confuse my mind. Tell me clearly the one way by which I can reach the highest good.

3.3 · Krishna

Gita 3.3

श्री भगवानुवाच
लोकेऽस्मिन्द्विविधा निष्ठा पुरा प्रोक्ता मयानघ ।
ज्ञानयोगेन सांख्यानां कर्मयोगेन योगिनाम् ॥

loke'smindvividhā niṣṭhā purā proktā mayānagha | jñānayogena sāṃkhyānāṃ karmayogena yoginām ||

Word by word
लोके अस्मिन्
in this world
द्वि-विधा निष्ठा
a twofold path
पुरा प्रोक्ता मया
was taught by me long ago
अनघ
O sinless one
ज्ञान-योगेन सांख्यानाम्
the path of knowledge for the contemplative
कर्म-योगेन योगिनाम्
the path of action for the active
Translation

The Lord said: As I said before, there are two paths in this world: the path of knowledge for those who reflect, and the path of action for those who work.

3.4 · Krishna

Gita 3.4

न कर्मणामनारम्भान्नैष्कर्म्यं पुरुषोऽश्नुते ।
न च संन्यसनादेव सिद्धिं समधिगच्छति ॥

na karmaṇāmanārambhānnaiṣkarmyaṃ puruṣo'śnute | na ca saṃnyasanādeva siddhiṃ samadhigacchati ||

Word by word
न कर्मणाम् अनारम्भात्
not by avoiding action
नैष्कर्म्यं
freedom from action
पुरुषः अश्नुते
does a person attain
न च संन्यसनात् एव
nor merely by giving up
सिद्धिं समधिगच्छति
does one reach perfection
Translation

A person does not become free from action just by not starting any work, nor reach perfection just by giving things up.

3.5 · Krishna

Gita 3.5

न हि कश्चित्क्षणमपि जातु तिष्ठत्यकर्मकृत् ।
कार्यते ह्यवशः कर्म सर्वः प्रकृतिजैर्गुणैः ॥

na hi kaścitkṣaṇamapi jātu tiṣṭhatyakarmakṛt | kāryate hyavaśaḥ karma sarvaḥ prakṛtijairguṇaiḥ ||

Word by word
न हि कश्चित्
no one at all
क्षणम् अपि
even for a moment
जातु तिष्ठति
ever remains
अकर्म-कृत्
without acting
कार्यते हि अवशः कर्म
everyone is made to act, helplessly
सर्वः
everyone
प्रकृति-जैः गुणैः
by the qualities born of nature
Translation

No one can stay without acting even for a moment; everyone is made to act, whether they like it or not, by the qualities of nature.

3.6 · Krishna

Gita 3.6

कर्मेन्द्रियाणि संयम्य य आस्ते मनसा स्मरन् ।
इन्द्रियार्थान्विमूढात्मा मिथ्याचारः स उच्यते ॥

karmendriyāṇi saṃyamya ya āste manasā smaran | indriyārthānvimūḍhātmā mithyācāraḥ sa ucyate ||

Word by word
कर्म-इन्द्रियाणि संयम्य
holding back the organs of action
यः आस्ते
one who sits
मनसा स्मरन्
dwelling in the mind
इन्द्रिय-अर्थान्
on sense objects
विमूढ-आत्मा
deluded
मिथ्या-आचारः
a hypocrite
सः उच्यते
is called
Translation

One who holds back the body from action but keeps thinking about pleasures in the mind is fooling himself and is called a hypocrite.

3.7 · Krishna

Gita 3.7

यस्त्विन्द्रियाणि मनसा नियम्यारभतेऽर्जुन ।
कर्मेन्द्रियैः कर्मयोगमसक्तः स विशिष्यते ॥

yastvindriyāṇi manasā niyamyārabhate'rjuna | karmendriyaiḥ karmayogamasaktaḥ sa viśiṣyate ||

Word by word
यः तु
but one who
इन्द्रियाणि मनसा नियम्य
controlling the senses with the mind
आरभते
undertakes
अर्जुन
O Arjuna
कर्म-इन्द्रियैः कर्म-योगम्
the yoga of action with the organs of action
असक्तः

Translation

But one who controls the senses with the mind and, without attachment, uses the body for selfless action, is far better, Arjuna.

3.8 · Krishna

Gita 3.8

नियतं कुरु कर्म त्वं कर्म ज्यायो ह्यकर्मणः ।
शरीरयात्रापि च ते न प्रसिद्ध्येदकर्मणः ॥

niyataṃ kuru karma tvaṃ karma jyāyo hyakarmaṇaḥ | śarīrayātrāpi ca te na prasiddhyedakarmaṇaḥ ||

Word by word
नियतं कुरु कर्म त्वं
do your prescribed work
कर्म ज्यायः हि अकर्मणः
action is better than inaction
शरीर-यात्रा अपि च ते
even the upkeep of your body
न प्रसिद्ध्येत् अकर्मणः
would not be possible without action
Translation

Do your duty, for action is better than inaction. Without action you could not even keep your body alive.

3.9 · Krishna

Gita 3.9

यज्ञार्थात्कर्मणोऽन्यत्र लोकोऽयं कर्मबन्धनः ।
तदर्थं कर्म कौन्तेय मुक्तसंगः समाचर ॥

yajñārthātkarmaṇo'nyatra loko'yaṃ karmabandhanaḥ | tadarthaṃ karma kaunteya muktasaṃgaḥ samācara ||

Word by word
यज्ञ-अर्थात् कर्मणः अन्यत्र
except for work done as an offering
लोकः अयं कर्म-बन्धनः
this world is bound by action
तत्-अर्थं कर्म
work for that purpose
कौन्तेय
O son of Kunti
मुक्त-सङ्गः समाचर
do it free from attachment
Translation

Action binds people, except action done as an offering (yajna). So, son of Kunti, work in that spirit, free from attachment.

📖 A story for this verse

An everyday story

Every evening at dinner, Rohan's father said, "No phones at the table." And every evening Rohan saw his father's own phone buzzing beside his plate, and his father glancing at it. Rohan kept sneaking looks at his phone too.

One day his father noticed this and understood. The next evening he put his own phone in a basket by the door before sitting down. He did not say a word about it.

Within a week, Rohan's phone was in the basket too. Then his little sister's tablet joined them. Dinner became the best talk of the day.

Children, teams and whole societies copy what their leaders do, not what they say. That is this verse.

📖 A story for this verse

A traditional folk tale

A crow saw a swan gliding on a lake and thought, "How graceful! I will walk like a swan." For days the crow practised the swan's slow, swaying walk. It never got it right, and the other birds laughed.

When the crow gave up and tried to walk like a crow again, it found it had forgotten how. Now it could walk neither way and hopped about awkwardly.

The crow had its own gifts: it was clever, quick and never went hungry. By chasing someone else's path, it lost its own.

The verse says your own path, even walked imperfectly, is better than someone else's path walked well.
