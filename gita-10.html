Divine Glories

Every verse of chapter 10, विभूतियोगः, with the Sanskrit, its pronunciation, the meaning of each word and a translation in simple English. Verses marked with a link are explained in depth on the main Gita page.

Verses 1–7: Krishna is the unborn source of the gods, the sages and every quality found in beings; 8–11: how the wise worship him, and what he gives them; 12–18: Arjuna accepts this and asks to hear his glories; 19–38: “Among these, I am this”, the best of every kind; 39–42: nothing exists without him, and all of it is one small part of him.

10.1 · Krishna

Gita 10.1

श्री भगवानुवाच
भूय एव महाबाहो शृणु मे परमं वचः ।
यत्तेऽहं प्रीयमाणाय वक्ष्यामि हितकाम्यया ॥

bhūya eva mahābāho śṛṇu me paramaṃ vacaḥ | yatte'haṃ prīyamāṇāya vakṣyāmi hitakāmyayā ||

Word by word
भूयः एव
once again
महा-बाहो
O mighty-armed (Arjuna)
शृणु मे परमं वचः
hear my highest word
यत् ते अहं वक्ष्यामि
which I shall tell you
प्रीयमाणाय
who take delight in it
हित-काम्यया
wishing your good
Translation

Krishna said: Arjuna, listen once more to my highest teaching. I shall tell it to you because you delight in it and because I wish you well.

10.2 · Krishna

Gita 10.2

न मे विदुः सुरगणाः प्रभवं न महर्षयः ।
अहमादिर्हि देवानां महर्षीणां च सर्वशः ॥

na me viduḥ suragaṇāḥ prabhavaṃ na maharṣayaḥ | ahamādirhi devānāṃ maharṣīṇāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ ||

Word by word
न मे विदुः प्रभवम्
do not know my origin
सुर-गणाः
the hosts of gods
न महर्षयः
nor the great sages
अहम् आदिः हि
for I am the source
देवानां महर्षीणां च
of the gods and the great sages
सर्वशः
in every way
Translation

Neither the hosts of gods nor the great sages know my origin, for I am the source of the gods and the great sages in every way.

10.3 · Krishna

Gita 10.3

यो मामजमनादिं च वेत्ति लोकमहेश्वरम् ।
असम्मूढः स मर्त्येषु सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥

yo māmajamanādiṃ ca vetti lokamaheśvaram | asammūḍhaḥ sa martyeṣu sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate ||

Word by word
यः मां वेत्ति
whoever knows me
अजम् अनादिं च
as unborn and without beginning
लोक-महेश्वरम्
the great Lord of the worlds
असम्मूढः सः मर्त्येषु
that person, undeluded among mortals
सर्व-पापैः प्रमुच्यते
is freed from all wrongs
Translation

Whoever knows me as unborn and without beginning, the great Lord of the worlds, is undeluded among mortals and is freed from all wrongs.

10.4 · Krishna

Gita 10.4

बुद्धिर्ज्ञानमसंमोहः क्षमा सत्यं दमः शमः ।
सुखं दुःखं भवोऽभावो भयं चाभयमेव च ॥

buddhirjñānamasaṃmohaḥ kṣamā satyaṃ damaḥ śamaḥ | sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ bhavo'bhāvo bhayaṃ cābhayameva ca ||

Word by word
बुद्धिः ज्ञानम्
intelligence, knowledge
असंमोहः
freedom from delusion
क्षमा सत्यम्
forgiveness, truthfulness
दमः शमः
self-control, calmness
सुखं दुःखम्
pleasure, pain
भवः अभावः
birth, death
भयं च अभयम् एव च
fear and also fearlessness
Translation

Intelligence, knowledge, freedom from delusion, forgiveness, truthfulness, self-control and calmness; pleasure and pain, birth and death, fear and fearlessness;

10.5 · Krishna

Gita 10.5

अहिंसा समता तुष्टिस्तपो दानं यशोऽयशः ।
भवन्ति भावा भूतानां मत्त एव पृथग्विधाः ॥

ahiṃsā samatā tuṣṭistapo dānaṃ yaśo'yaśaḥ | bhavanti bhāvā bhūtānāṃ matta eva pṛthagvidhāḥ ||

Word by word
अहिंसा समता तुष्टिः
non-violence, even-mindedness, contentment
तपः दानम्
discipline, giving
यशः अयशः
fame, ill fame
भवन्ति भावाः भूतानाम्
these states of beings arise
मत्तः एव
from me alone
पृथक्-विधाः
in their different kinds
Translation

non-violence, even-mindedness, contentment, self-discipline, giving, fame and ill fame: all these different states of beings come from me alone.

10.6 · Krishna

Gita 10.6

महर्षयः सप्त पूर्वे चत्वारो मनवस्तथा ।
मद्भावा मानसा जाता येषां लोक इमाः प्रजाः ॥

maharṣayaḥ sapta pūrve catvāro manavastathā | madbhāvā mānasā jātā yeṣāṃ loka imāḥ prajāḥ ||

Word by word
महर्षयः सप्त
the seven great sages
पूर्वे चत्वारः
the four before them
मनवः तथा
and also the Manus
मत्-भावाः
have my nature
मानसाः जाताः
born from my mind
येषां लोके इमाः प्रजाः
from whom all the people in the world have come
Translation

The seven great sages, the four who came before them, and the Manus all have my nature and were born from my mind. From them have come all the people of the world.

10.7 · Krishna

Gita 10.7

एतां विभूतिं योगं च मम यो वेत्ति तत्त्वतः ।
सोऽविकम्पेन योगेन युज्यते नात्र संशयः ॥

etāṃ vibhūtiṃ yogaṃ ca mama yo vetti tattvataḥ | so'vikampena yogena yujyate nātra saṃśayaḥ ||

Word by word
एतां विभूतिं योगं च मम
this glory and power of mine
यः वेत्ति तत्त्वतः
whoever truly knows
सः अविकम्पेन योगेन युज्यते
is united with me in unshakeable yoga
न अत्र संशयः
there is no doubt of this
Translation

Whoever truly knows this glory and power of mine is united with me in a yoga that cannot be shaken. There is no doubt of this.

To those who are always united with me and worship me with love, I give the understanding by which they come to me.

10.12 · Arjuna

Gita 10.12

अर्जुन उवाच
परं ब्रह्म परं धाम पवित्रं परमं भवान् ।

Word by word
परं ब्रह्म

Arjuna said: You are the supreme Brahman, the supreme home and the supreme purifier. The eternal divine Person, the first of the gods, unborn and present everywhere:

10.21 · Krishna

10.22 · Krishna

Gita 10.22
