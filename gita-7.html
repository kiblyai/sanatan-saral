Chapter 7 · 30 verses

The Yoga of Knowledge and Realisation

Every verse of chapter 7, ज्ञानविज्ञानयोगः, with the Sanskrit, its pronunciation, the meaning of each word and a translation in simple English. Verses marked with a link are explained in depth on the main Gita page.

Verses 1–3: Krishna promises full knowledge, and says how rare it is to truly know him; 4–7: his two natures, with everything strung on him like pearls on a thread; 8–12: where to see him in everyday things; 13–15: maya, and why people fail to see him; 16–19: the four kinds of people who worship him; 20–23: the worship of other gods; 24–30: the unseen Krishna, and those who come to know him.

7.1 · Krishna

Gita 7.1

श्री भगवानुवाच
मय्यासक्तमनाः पार्थ योगं युञ्जन्मदाश्रयः ।
असंशयं समग्रं मां यथा ज्ञास्यसि तच्छृणु ॥

mayyāsaktamanāḥ pārtha yogaṃ yuñjanmadāśrayaḥ | asaṃśayaṃ samagraṃ māṃ yathā jñāsyasi tacchṛṇu ||

Word by word
मयि आसक्त-मनाः
with your mind attached to me
पार्थ
O Partha (Arjuna)
योगं युञ्जन्
practising yoga
मत्-आश्रयः
taking shelter in me
असंशयं समग्रं माम्
me fully, without doubt
यथा ज्ञास्यसि
how you will know
तत् शृणु
hear that
Translation

Krishna said: Arjuna, hear how you will come to know me fully and without doubt, by practising yoga with your mind fixed on me and taking shelter in me.

7.2 · Krishna

Gita 7.2

ज्ञानं तेऽहं सविज्ञानमिदं वक्ष्याम्यशेषतः ।
यज्ज्ञात्वा नेह भूयोऽन्यज्ज्ञातव्यमवशिष्यते ॥

jñānaṃ te'haṃ savijñānamidaṃ vakṣyāmyaśeṣataḥ | yajjñātvā neha bhūyo'nyajjñātavyamavaśiṣyate ||

Word by word
ज्ञानं स-विज्ञानम्
knowledge together with direct experience
ते अहं वक्ष्यामि
I shall tell you
इदम् अशेषतः
this, in full
यत् ज्ञात्वा
knowing which
न इह भूयः अन्यत्
nothing more here
ज्ञातव्यम् अवशिष्यते
remains to be known
Translation

I shall teach you this knowledge in full, along with the direct experience of it. Once you know it, nothing more remains to be known here.

7.3 · Krishna

Gita 7.3

मनुष्याणां सहस्रेषु कश्चिद्यतति सिद्धये ।
यततामपि सिद्धानां कश्चिन्मां वेत्ति तत्त्वतः ॥

manuṣyāṇāṃ sahasreṣu kaścidyatati siddhaye | yatatāmapi siddhānāṃ kaścinmāṃ vetti tattvataḥ ||

Word by word
मनुष्याणां सहस्रेषु
among thousands of people
कश्चित् यतति
hardly one strives
सिद्धये
for perfection
यतताम् अपि सिद्धानाम्
and even among those who strive and succeed
कश्चित् मां वेत्ति
hardly one knows me
तत्त्वतः
as I truly am
Translation

Among thousands of people, hardly one strives for perfection. And even among those who strive and succeed, hardly one knows me as I truly am.

7.4 · Krishna

Gita 7.4

भूमिरापोऽनलो वायुः खं मनो बुद्धिरेव च ।
अहङ्कार इतीयं मे भिन्ना प्रकृतिरष्टधा ॥

bhūmirāpo'nalo vāyuḥ khaṃ mano buddhireva ca | ahaṅkāra itīyaṃ me bhinnā prakṛtiraṣṭadhā ||

Word by word
भूमिः आपः
earth, water
अनलः वायुः खम्
fire, air, space
मनः बुद्धिः एव च
mind and intellect too
अहङ्कारः
the sense of "I" (ego)
इति इयं मे प्रकृतिः
this is my nature
भिन्ना अष्टधा
divided into eight parts
Translation

Earth, water, fire, air, space, mind, intellect and ego: this is my nature, divided into eight parts.

7.5 · Krishna

Gita 7.5

अपरेयमितस्त्वन्यां प्रकृतिं विद्धि मे पराम् ।
जीवभूतां महाबाहो ययेदं धार्यते जगत् ॥

apareyamitastvanyāṃ prakṛtiṃ viddhi me parām | jīvabhūtāṃ mahābāho yayedaṃ dhāryate jagat ||

Word by word
अपरा इयम्
this is the lower one
इतः तु अन्याम्
but different from this
प्रकृतिं विद्धि मे पराम्
know my higher nature
जीव-भूताम्
which is the life in beings
महा-बाहो
O mighty-armed (Arjuna)
यया इदं धार्यते जगत्
by which this world is held up
Translation

That is my lower nature. Know that I have another, higher nature, Arjuna: the life in all beings, by which this whole world is held up.

7.6 · Krishna

Gita 7.6

एतद्योनीनि भूतानि सर्वाणीत्युपधारय ।

Word by word
एतत्-योनीनि
born from these two
भूतानि सर्वाणि

इति उपधारय

अहं कृत्स्नस्य जगतः

प्रभवः

Understand that all beings are born from these two natures. I am the origin of the whole world, and I am also its end.

Gita 7.7

7.8 · Krishna

Gita 7.8

रसोऽहमप्सु कौन्तेय प्रभास्मि शशिसूर्ययोः ।
प्रणवः सर्ववेदेषु शब्दः खे पौरुषं नृषु ॥

raso'hamapsu kaunteya prabhāsmi śaśisūryayoḥ | praṇavaḥ sarvavedeṣu śabdaḥ khe pauruṣaṃ nṛṣu ||

Word by word
रसः अहम् अप्सु
I am the taste in water
कौन्तेय
O son of Kunti (Arjuna)
प्रभा अस्मि शशि-सूर्ययोः
I am the light of the moon and the sun
प्रणवः सर्व-वेदेषु
the syllable Om in all the Vedas
शब्दः खे
the sound in space
पौरुषं नृषु
the ability in people
Translation

Arjuna, I am the taste in water and the light of the moon and the sun. I am Om in all the Vedas, the sound in space and the ability in people.

and
तेजः च अस्मि विभावसौ

Gita 7.10

बीजं मां सर्वभूतानां विद्धि पार्थ सनातनम् ।

बीजं मां सर्व-भूतानाम्

7.11 · Krishna

Gita 7.11

बलं बलवतां चाहं कामरागविवर्जितम् ।
धर्माविरुद्धो भूतेषु कामोऽस्मि भरतर्षभ ॥

balaṃ balavatāṃ cāhaṃ kāmarāgavivarjitam | dharmāviruddho bhūteṣu kāmo'smi bharatarṣabha ||

Word by word
बलं बलवतां च अहम्
I am the strength of the strong
काम-राग-विवर्जितम्
free from craving and attachment
धर्म-अविरुद्धः

7.12 · Krishna

Gita 7.12

ये चैव सात्त्विका भावा राजसास्तामसाश्च ये ।
मत्त एवेति तान्विद्धि नत्वहं तेषु ते मयि ॥

ye caiva sāttvikā bhāvā rājasāstāmasāśca ye | matta eveti tānviddhi natvahaṃ teṣu te mayi ||

Word by word
ये च एव सात्त्विकाः भावाः
whatever states are of goodness (sattva)
राजसाः तामसाः च ये
and those of passion (rajas) and dullness (tamas)
मत्तः एव इति तान् विद्धि
know that they come from me alone
न तु अहं तेषु
yet I am not in them
ते मयि
they are in me
Translation

Whatever states arise from goodness, passion or dullness, know that they all come from me. Yet I am not in them; they are in me.

Wrongdoers who are deluded, the lowest among people, do not take refuge in me. Maya has carried away their understanding, and they have taken up a demonic nature.

7.21 · Krishna
