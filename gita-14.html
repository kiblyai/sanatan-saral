Chapter 14 · 27 verses

The Three Gunas

Every verse of chapter 14, गुणत्रयविभागयोगः, with the Sanskrit, its pronunciation, the meaning of each word and a translation in simple English. Verses marked with a link are explained in depth on the main Gita page.

Verses 1–4: the highest knowledge, with nature as the womb and Krishna as the father of all beings; 5–9: the three gunas, goodness, passion and dullness, and how each one binds; 10–13: how to tell which guna is strongest; 14–18: where each guna leads, at death and after; 19–20: going beyond the gunas; 21–27: the signs of a person who has gone beyond them, and devotion as the way.

14.1 · Krishna

Gita 14.1

श्री भगवानुवाच
परं भूयः प्रवक्ष्यामि ज्ञानानां ज्ञानमुत्तमम् ।
यज्ज्ञात्वा मुनयः सर्वे परां सिद्धिमितो गताः ॥

paraṃ bhūyaḥ pravakṣyāmi jñānānāṃ jñānamuttamam | yajjñātvā munayaḥ sarve parāṃ siddhimito gatāḥ ||

Word by word
परं भूयः प्रवक्ष्यामि
I shall tell you again the highest
ज्ञानानां ज्ञानम् उत्तमम्
the best of all knowledge
यत् ज्ञात्वा
knowing which
मुनयः सर्वे
all the sages
परां सिद्धिम् इतः गताः
have gone from here to the highest perfection
Translation

Krishna said: I shall tell you once more the highest knowledge, the best of all knowledge. Knowing it, all the sages have gone from this world to the highest perfection.

14.2 · Krishna

Gita 14.2

इदं ज्ञानमुपाश्रित्य मम साधर्म्यमागताः ।
सर्गेऽपि नोपजायन्ते प्रलये न व्यथन्ति च ॥

idaṃ jñānamupāśritya mama sādharmyamāgatāḥ | sarge'pi nopajāyante pralaye na vyathanti ca ||

Word by word
इदं ज्ञानम् उपाश्रित्य
taking refuge in this knowledge
मम साधर्म्यम् आगताः
they have come to share my nature
सर्गे अपि न उपजायन्ते
they are not born even at creation
प्रलये न व्यथन्ति च
and are not troubled at the dissolution
Translation

Those who take refuge in this knowledge come to share my nature. They are not born when the world is created, and they are not troubled when it dissolves.

14.3 · Krishna

Gita 14.3

मम योनिर्महद्ब्रह्म तस्मिन् गर्भं दधाम्यहम् ।
संभवः सर्वभूतानां ततो भवति भारत ॥

mama yonirmahadbrahma tasmin garbhaṃ dadhāmyaham | saṃbhavaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ tato bhavati bhārata ||

Word by word
मम योनिः महत् ब्रह्म
great nature is my womb
तस्मिन् गर्भं दधामि अहम्
in it I place the seed
सम्भवः सर्व-भूतानाम्
the birth of all beings
ततः भवति
comes from that
भारत
O descendant of Bharata (Arjuna)
Translation

Great nature is my womb. In it I place the seed, and from that, Arjuna, all beings are born.

14.4 · Krishna

Gita 14.4

सर्वयोनिषु कौन्तेय मूर्तयः सम्भवन्ति याः ।
तासां ब्रह्म महद्योनिरहं बीजप्रदः पिता ॥

sarvayoniṣu kaunteya mūrtayaḥ sambhavanti yāḥ | tāsāṃ brahma mahadyonirahaṃ bījapradaḥ pitā ||

Word by word
सर्व-योनिषु
in all wombs
कौन्तेय
O son of Kunti (Arjuna)
मूर्तयः सम्भवन्ति याः
whatever forms are born
तासां ब्रह्म महत् योनिः
great nature is their womb
अहं बीज-प्रदः पिता
and I am the father who gives the seed
Translation

Whatever forms are born in any womb, Arjuna, great nature is their womb and I am the father who gives the seed.

14.5 · Krishna

Gita 14.5

सत्त्वं रजस्तम इति गुणाः प्रकृतिसंभवाः ।
निबध्नन्ति महाबाहो देहे देहिनमव्ययम् ॥

sattvaṃ rajastama iti guṇāḥ prakṛtisaṃbhavāḥ | nibadhnanti mahābāho dehe dehinamavyayam ||

Word by word
सत्त्वं रजः तमः इति
goodness (sattva), passion (rajas) and dullness (tamas)
गुणाः प्रकृति-सम्भवाः
these gunas, born of nature
निबध्नन्ति
bind
महा-बाहो
O mighty-armed (Arjuna)
देहे देहिनम् अव्ययम्
the unchanging soul to the body
Translation

Goodness, passion and dullness, the three gunas born of nature, bind the unchanging soul to the body, Arjuna.

14.6 · Krishna

Gita 14.6

तत्र सत्त्वं निर्मलत्वात्प्रकाशकमनामयम् ।
सुखसङ्गेन बध्नाति ज्ञानसङ्गेन चानघ ॥

tatra sattvaṃ nirmalatvātprakāśakamanāmayam | sukhasaṅgena badhnāti jñānasaṅgena cānagha ||

Word by word
तत्र सत्त्वं निर्मलत्वात्
of these, goodness, being pure
प्रकाशकम् अनामयम्
gives light and is healthy
सुख-सङ्गेन बध्नाति
it binds by attachment to happiness
ज्ञान-सङ्गेन च
and by attachment to knowledge
अनघ
O sinless one (Arjuna)
Translation

Of these, goodness is pure, and so it gives light and health. It binds by attachment to happiness and to knowledge, Arjuna.

14.7 · Krishna

Gita 14.7

रजो रागात्मकं विद्धि तृष्णासङ्गसमुद्भवम् ।
तन्निबध्नाति कौन्तेय कर्मसङ्गेन देहिनम् ॥

rajo rāgātmakaṃ viddhi tṛṣṇāsaṅgasamudbhavam | tannibadhnāti kaunteya karmasaṅgena dehinam ||

Word by word
रजः राग-आत्मकं विद्धि
know that passion is of the nature of desire
तृष्णा-सङ्ग-समुद्भवम्
it gives rise to craving and attachment
तत् निबध्नाति देहिनम्
it binds the soul
कौन्तेय
O son of Kunti (Arjuna)
कर्म-सङ्गेन
by attachment to action
Translation

Know that passion is of the nature of desire, and gives rise to craving and attachment. It binds the soul, Arjuna, by attachment to action.

14.8 · Krishna

Gita 14.8

तमस्त्वज्ञानजं विद्धि मोहनं सर्वदेहिनाम् ।
प्रमादालस्यनिद्राभिस्तन्निबध्नाति भारत ॥

tamastvajñānajaṃ viddhi mohanaṃ sarvadehinām | pramādālasyanidrābhistannibadhnāti bhārata ||

Word by word
तमः तु अज्ञान-जं विद्धि
but know that dullness is born of ignorance
मोहनं सर्व-देहिनाम्
it deludes all embodied beings
प्रमाद-आलस्य-निद्राभिः
by carelessness, laziness and sleep
तत् निबध्नाति
it binds
भारत
O descendant of Bharata (Arjuna)
Translation

But know that dullness is born of ignorance and deludes all beings. It binds, Arjuna, by carelessness, laziness and sleep.

14.9 · Krishna

Gita 14.9

सत्त्वं सुखे सञ्जयति रजः कर्मणि भारत ।
ज्ञानमावृत्य तु तमः प्रमादे सञ्जयत्युत ॥

sattvaṃ sukhe sañjayati rajaḥ karmaṇi bhārata | jñānamāvṛtya tu tamaḥ pramāde sañjayatyuta ||

Word by word
सत्त्वं सुखे सञ्जयति
goodness ties a person to happiness
रजः कर्मणि
passion to action
भारत
O descendant of Bharata (Arjuna)
ज्ञानम् आवृत्य तु तमः
but dullness, covering knowledge
प्रमादे सञ्जयति उत
ties a person to carelessness
Translation

Goodness ties a person to happiness, Arjuna, and passion ties a person to action. But dullness covers knowledge and ties a person to carelessness.

14.10 · Krishna

Gita 14.10

रजस्तमश्चाभिभूय सत्त्वं भवति भारत ।
रजः सत्त्वं तमश्चैव तमः सत्त्वं रजस्तथा ॥

rajastamaścābhibhūya sattvaṃ bhavati bhārata | rajaḥ sattvaṃ tamaścaiva tamaḥ sattvaṃ rajastathā ||

Word by word
रजः तमः च अभिभूय
overpowering passion and dullness
सत्त्वं भवति
goodness rises
भारत
O descendant of Bharata (Arjuna)
रजः सत्त्वं तमः च एव
passion rises over goodness and dullness
तमः सत्त्वं रजः तथा
and dullness over goodness and passion
Translation

Sometimes goodness rises, Arjuna, overpowering passion and dullness. Sometimes passion overpowers goodness and dullness, and sometimes dullness overpowers goodness and passion.

14.11 · Krishna

Gita 14.11

सर्वद्वारेषु देहेऽस्मिन्प्रकाश उपजायते ।
ज्ञानं यदा तदा विद्याद्विवृद्धं सत्त्वमित्युत ॥

sarvadvāreṣu dehe'sminprakāśa upajāyate | jñānaṃ yadā tadā vidyādvivṛddhaṃ sattvamityuta ||

Word by word
सर्व-द्वारेषु देहे अस्मिन्
in all the gates of this body
प्रकाशः उपजायते ज्ञानम्
the light of knowledge shines
यदा
when
तदा विद्यात्
then one should know
विवृद्धं सत्त्वम् इति उत
that goodness has grown strong
Translation

When the light of knowledge shines through all the gates of this body, know that goodness has grown strong.

14.14 · Krishna

Gita 14.14

यदा सत्त्वे प्रवृद्धे तु प्रलयं याति देहभृत् ।
तदोत्तमविदां लोकानमलान्प्रतिपद्यते ॥

yadā sattve pravṛddhe tu pralayaṃ yāti dehabhṛt | tadottamavidāṃ lokānamalānpratipadyate ||

Word by word
यदा सत्त्वे प्रवृद्धे तु

प्रलयं याति देह-भृत्
and the embodied soul meets death
तदा उत्तम-विदां लोकान् अमलान्
then the pure worlds of those who know the highest
प्रतिपद्यते
it reaches
Translation

If a person dies when goodness is strong, that soul reaches the pure worlds of those who know the highest.

14.15 · Krishna

Gita 14.15

रजसि प्रलयं गत्वा कर्मसङ्गिषु जायते ।
तथा प्रलीनस्तमसि मूढयोनिषु जायते ॥

rajasi pralayaṃ gatvā karmasaṅgiṣu jāyate | tathā pralīnastamasi mūḍhayoniṣu jāyate ||

Word by word
रजसि प्रलयं गत्वा
dying when passion is strong
कर्म-सङ्गिषु जायते
one is born among people attached to action
तथा प्रलीनः तमसि
and dying in dullness
मूढ-योनिषु जायते
one is born among the unthinking
Translation

One who dies when passion is strong is born among people attached to action. One who dies in dullness is born among creatures without understanding.

14.16 · Krishna

Gita 14.16

कर्मणः सुकृतस्याहुः सात्त्विकं निर्मलं फलम् ।
रजसस्तु फलं दुःखमज्ञानं तमसः फलम् ॥

karmaṇaḥ sukṛtasyāhuḥ sāttvikaṃ nirmalaṃ phalam | rajasastu phalaṃ duḥkhamajñānaṃ tamasaḥ phalam ||

Word by word
कर्मणः सुकृतस्य आहुः
they say that of good action
सात्त्विकं निर्मलं फलम्
the fruit is pure and of goodness
रजसः तु फलं दुःखम्
but the fruit of passion is pain
अज्ञानं तमसः फलम्
and the fruit of dullness is ignorance
Translation

They say that the fruit of good action is pure and belongs to goodness. The fruit of passion is pain, and the fruit of dullness is ignorance.

14.17 · Krishna

Gita 14.17

सत्त्वात्सञ्जायते ज्ञानं रजसो लोभ एव च ।
प्रमादमोहौ तमसो भवतोऽज्ञानमेव च ॥

sattvātsañjāyate jñānaṃ rajaso lobha eva ca | pramādamohau tamaso bhavato'jñānameva ca ||

Word by word
सत्त्वात् सञ्जायते ज्ञानम्
from goodness comes knowledge
रजसः लोभः एव च
from passion, greed
प्रमाद-मोहौ तमसः भवतः
from dullness come carelessness and delusion
अज्ञानम् एव च
and also ignorance
Translation

From goodness comes knowledge, and from passion comes greed. From dullness come carelessness, delusion and ignorance.

14.18 · Krishna

Gita 14.18

ऊर्ध्वं गच्छन्ति सत्त्वस्था मध्ये तिष्ठन्ति राजसाः ।
जघन्यगुणवृत्तिस्था अधो गच्छन्ति तामसाः ॥

ūrdhvaṃ gacchanti sattvasthā madhye tiṣṭhanti rājasāḥ | jaghanyaguṇavṛttisthā adho gacchanti tāmasāḥ ||

Word by word
ऊर्ध्वं गच्छन्ति सत्त्व-स्थाः
those settled in goodness go upward
मध्ये तिष्ठन्ति राजसाः
those of passion stay in the middle
जघन्य-गुण-वृत्ति-स्थाः
settled in the ways of the lowest guna
अधः गच्छन्ति तामसाः
those of dullness go downward
Translation

Those settled in goodness go upward. Those of passion stay in the middle. Those of dullness, settled in the ways of the lowest guna, go downward.

14.19 · Krishna

Gita 14.19

When the one who sees perceives that there is no doer other than the gunas, and knows what is beyond the gunas, that person reaches my state.

14.20 · Krishna

Gita 14.20

गुणानेतानतीत्य त्रीन्देही देहसमुद्भवान् ।

guṇānetānatītya trīndehī dehasamudbhavān | janmamṛtyujarāduḥkhairvimukto'mṛtamaśnute ||

14.21 · Arjuna

Gita 14.21

अर्जुन उवाच
कैर्लिङ्गैस्त्रीन्गुणानेतानतीतो भवति प्रभो ।
किमाचारः कथं चैतांस्त्रीन्गुणानतिवर्तते ॥

kairliṅgaistrīnguṇānetānatīto bhavati prabho | kimācāraḥ kathaṃ caitāṃstrīnguṇānativartate ||

Word by word
कैः लिङ्गैः
by what signs
त्रीन् गुणान् एतान् अतीतः भवति
is one known to have gone beyond these three gunas
प्रभो
O Lord
किम्-आचारः
how does such a person behave
कथं च एतान् त्रीन् गुणान् अतिवर्तते
and how does one go beyond these three gunas
Translation

Arjuna asked: Lord, what are the signs of a person who has gone beyond these three gunas? How does such a person behave, and how does one go beyond the three gunas?

14.22 · Krishna

Gita 14.22

श्री भगवानुवाच
प्रकाशं च प्रवृत्तिं च मोहमेव च पाण्डव ।
न द्वेष्टि सम्प्रवृत्तानि न निवृत्तानि काङ्क्षति ॥

prakāśaṃ ca pravṛttiṃ ca mohameva ca pāṇḍava | na dveṣṭi sampravṛttāni na nivṛttāni kāṅkṣati ||

Word by word
प्रकाशं च प्रवृत्तिं च मोहम् एव च

पाण्डव
O son of Pandu (Arjuna)
न द्वेष्टि सम्प्रवृत्तानि
one does not hate them when they are present
न निवृत्तानि काङ्क्षति

Translation

Krishna said: Such a person, Arjuna, does not hate light, activity or delusion when they are present, and does not long for them when they are gone.

Such a person sits like one who is not involved and is not shaken by the gunas. Knowing that it is only the gunas that act, that person stands firm and does not waver.
