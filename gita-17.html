Chapter 17 · 28 verses

The Three Kinds of Faith

Every verse of chapter 17, श्रद्धात्रयविभागयोगः, with the Sanskrit, its pronunciation, the meaning of each word and a translation in simple English. Verses marked with a link are explained in depth on the main Gita page.

Verses 1–6: Arjuna's question, and the three kinds of faith; 7–10: three kinds of food; 11–13: three kinds of sacrifice; 14–19: discipline of body, speech and mind, and its three kinds; 20–22: three kinds of giving; 23–28: “Om Tat Sat”, and why nothing done without faith has value.

17.1 · Arjuna

Gita 17.1

अर्जुन उवाच
ये शास्त्रविधिमुत्सृज्य यजन्ते श्रद्धयाऽन्विताः ।
तेषां निष्ठा तु का कृष्ण सत्त्वमाहो रजस्तमः ॥

ye śāstravidhimutsṛjya yajante śraddhayā'nvitāḥ | teṣāṃ niṣṭhā tu kā kṛṣṇa sattvamāho rajastamaḥ ||

Word by word
ये शास्त्र-विधिम् उत्सृज्य
those who set aside the guidance of the scriptures
यजन्ते श्रद्धया अन्विताः
yet worship with faith
तेषां निष्ठा तु का
what is their standing
कृष्ण
O Krishna
सत्त्वम् आहो रजः तमः
is it goodness, or passion, or dullness
Translation

Arjuna asked: Krishna, what of those who set aside the guidance of the scriptures and yet worship with faith? Is their standing one of goodness, of passion or of dullness?

17.2 · Krishna

Gita 17.2

श्री भगवानुवाच
त्रिविधा भवति श्रद्धा देहिनां सा स्वभावजा ।
सात्त्विकी राजसी चैव तामसी चेति तां शृणु ॥

trividhā bhavati śraddhā dehināṃ sā svabhāvajā | sāttvikī rājasī caiva tāmasī ceti tāṃ śṛṇu ||

Word by word
त्रिविधा भवति श्रद्धा
faith is of three kinds
देहिनां सा स्वभाव-जा
in embodied beings it is born of their own nature
सात्त्विकी राजसी च एव तामसी च इति
of goodness, of passion and of dullness
तां शृणु
hear about it
Translation

Krishna said: The faith of people is of three kinds, born of their own nature: of goodness, of passion and of dullness. Hear about it.

17.3 · Krishna

Gita 17.3

सत्त्वानुरूपा सर्वस्य श्रद्धा भवति भारत ।
श्रद्धामयोऽयं पुरुषो यो यच्छ्रद्धः स एव सः ॥

sattvānurūpā sarvasya śraddhā bhavati bhārata | śraddhāmayo'yaṃ puruṣo yo yacchraddhaḥ sa eva saḥ ||

Word by word
सत्त्व-अनुरूपा सर्वस्य श्रद्धा भवति
everyone's faith follows their inner nature
भारत
O descendant of Bharata (Arjuna)
श्रद्धा-मयः अयं पुरुषः
a person is made of faith
यः यत्-श्रद्धः सः एव सः
whatever one's faith is, that is what one is
Translation

Everyone's faith follows their inner nature, Arjuna. A person is made of faith: whatever a person's faith is, that is what the person is.

17.4 · Krishna

Gita 17.4

यजन्ते सात्त्विका देवान्यक्षरक्षांसि राजसाः ।
प्रेतान्भूतगणांश्चान्ये यजन्ते तामसा जनाः ॥

yajante sāttvikā devānyakṣarakṣāṃsi rājasāḥ | pretānbhūtagaṇāṃścānye yajante tāmasā janāḥ ||

Word by word
यजन्ते सात्त्विकाः देवान्
people of goodness worship the gods
यक्ष-रक्षांसि राजसाः
people of passion worship yakshas and rakshasas
प्रेतान् भूत-गणान् च अन्ये यजन्ते तामसाः जनाः
the others, people of dullness, worship ghosts and spirits
Translation

People of goodness worship the gods. People of passion worship yakshas and rakshasas. The others, people of dullness, worship ghosts and spirits.

Gita 17.5

Word by word
अशास्त्र-विहितं घोरं तपः
harsh disciplines not taught in the scriptures
तप्यन्ते ये जनाः
the people who practise
दम्भ-अहङ्कार-संयुक्ताः

People who practise harsh disciplines not taught in the scriptures, out of pretence and ego, driven by the force of desire and attachment,

17.6 · Krishna

Gita 17.6

कर्षयन्तः शरीरस्थं भूतग्राममचेतसः ।
मां चैवान्तःशरीरस्थं तान्विद्ध्यासुरनिश्चयान् ॥

karṣayantaḥ śarīrasthaṃ bhūtagrāmamacetasaḥ | māṃ caivāntaḥśarīrasthaṃ tānviddhyāsuraniścayān ||

Word by word
कर्षयन्तः शरीर-स्थं भूत-ग्रामम्
torturing the elements of the body
अचेतसः
without understanding
मां च एव अन्तः-शरीर-स्थम्
and me too, who live within the body
तान् विद्धि आसुर-निश्चयान्
know them to be demonic in their resolve
Translation

who senselessly torture the elements of the body, and me too, who live within the body: know them to be demonic in their resolve.

17.7 · Krishna

Gita 17.7

आहारस्त्वपि सर्वस्य त्रिविधो भवति प्रियः ।
यज्ञस्तपस्तथा दानं तेषां भेदमिमं शृणु ॥

āhārastvapi sarvasya trividho bhavati priyaḥ | yajñastapastathā dānaṃ teṣāṃ bhedamimaṃ śṛṇu ||

Word by word
आहारः तु अपि सर्वस्य त्रिविधः भवति प्रियः
the food that each person likes is also of three kinds
यज्ञः तपः तथा दानम्
and so are sacrifice, discipline and giving
तेषां भेदम् इमं शृणु
hear how they differ
Translation

The food that people like is also of three kinds, and so are sacrifice, self-discipline and giving. Hear how they differ.

17.8 · Krishna

Gita 17.8

आयुःसत्त्वबलारोग्यसुखप्रीतिविवर्धनाः ।
रस्याः स्निग्धाः स्थिरा हृद्या आहाराः सात्त्विकप्रियाः ॥

āyuḥsattvabalārogyasukhaprītivivardhanāḥ | rasyāḥ snigdhāḥ sthirā hṛdyā āhārāḥ sāttvikapriyāḥ ||

Word by word
आयुः-सत्त्व-बल-आरोग्य-सुख-प्रीति-विवर्धनाः
which increase life, energy, strength, health, happiness and satisfaction
रस्याः स्निग्धाः
juicy and smooth
स्थिराः हृद्याः
nourishing and pleasing to the heart
आहाराः सात्त्विक-प्रियाः
such foods are dear to people of goodness
Translation

Foods that increase life, energy, strength, health, happiness and satisfaction, and that are juicy, smooth, nourishing and pleasing, are dear to people of goodness.

17.9 · Krishna

Gita 17.9

कट्वम्ललवणात्युष्णतीक्ष्णरूक्षविदाहिनः ।
आहारा राजसस्येष्टा दुःखशोकामयप्रदाः ॥

kaṭvamlalavaṇātyuṣṇatīkṣṇarūkṣavidāhinaḥ | āhārā rājasasyeṣṭā duḥkhaśokāmayapradāḥ ||

Word by word
कटु-अम्ल-लवण-अति-उष्ण-तीक्ष्ण-रूक्ष-विदाहिनः
bitter, sour, salty, very hot, sharp, dry and burning
आहाराः राजसस्य इष्टाः
such foods are liked by people of passion
दुःख-शोक-आमय-प्रदाः
they bring pain, sorrow and illness
Translation

Foods that are bitter, sour, salty, very hot, sharp, dry and burning are liked by people of passion. They bring pain, sorrow and illness.

Gita 17.10

यातयामं गतरसं पूति पर्युषितं च यत् ।

yātayāmaṃ gatarasaṃ pūti paryuṣitaṃ ca yat | ucchiṣṭamapi cāmedhyaṃ bhojanaṃ tāmasapriyam ||

Word by word

stale
गत-रसम्

पूति
rotten
पर्युषितं च यत्

17.11 · Krishna

Gita 17.11

अफलाकाङ्क्षिभिर्यज्ञो विधिदृष्टो य इज्यते ।
यष्टव्यमेवेति मनः समाधाय स सात्त्विकः ॥

aphalākāṅkṣibhiryajño vidhidṛṣṭo ya ijyate | yaṣṭavyameveti manaḥ samādhāya sa sāttvikaḥ ||

Word by word
अफल-आकाङ्क्षिभिः
by those who want no reward
यज्ञः विधि-दृष्टः यः इज्यते
the sacrifice that is offered as the scriptures teach
यष्टव्यम् एव इति मनः समाधाय
with the mind settled that “this ought to be offered”
सः सात्त्विकः
that is of goodness
Translation

A sacrifice offered as the scriptures teach, by people who want no reward and whose minds are settled on the thought that it simply ought to be offered, is of goodness.

17.12 · Krishna

Gita 17.12

अभिसंधाय तु फलं दम्भार्थमपि चैव यत् ।

But a sacrifice offered with a reward in view, or for show, Arjuna, know to be of passion.

17.13 · Krishna

Gita 17.13

विधिहीनमसृष्टान्नं मन्त्रहीनमदक्षिणम् ।
श्रद्धाविरहितं यज्ञं तामसं परिचक्षते ॥

vidhihīnamasṛṣṭānnaṃ mantrahīnamadakṣiṇam | śraddhāvirahitaṃ yajñaṃ tāmasaṃ paricakṣate ||

Word by word
विधि-हीनम्
not following the scriptures
असृष्ट-अन्नम्

without mantras
अदक्षिणम्
without gifts to the priests
श्रद्धा-विरहितं यज्ञम्

A sacrifice that does not follow the scriptures, in which no food is given out, no mantras are chanted and no gifts are given, and which is empty of faith, is said to be of dullness.

Gita 17.14

Honouring the gods, the brahmins, teachers and the wise; purity, honesty, self-restraint and non-violence: this is called the discipline of the body.

Gita 17.17

A gift given because it is right to give, to someone who can do nothing in return, at the right place and time and to a worthy person, is held to be of goodness.

17.21 · Krishna

Gita 17.21

यत्तु प्रत्युपकारार्थं फलमुद्दिश्य वा पुनः ।
