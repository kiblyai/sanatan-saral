Gita chapter 18: Liberation through Renunciation

Bhagavad Gita 18.42: śamo damastapaḥ śaucaṃ kṣāntirārjavameva ca

Calmness, self-control, self-discipline, purity, patience, honesty, knowledge, direct experience and faith are the duties of a brahmin, born of his nature.

Gita 18.42

शमो दमस्तपः शौचं क्षान्तिरार्जवमेव च ।
ज्ञानं विज्ञानमास्तिक्यं ब्रह्मकर्म स्वभावजम् ॥

śamo damastapaḥ śaucaṃ kṣāntirārjavameva ca | jñānaṃ vijñānamāstikyaṃ brahmakarma svabhāvajam ||

Word by word
शमः दमः तपः शौचम्
calmness, self-control, discipline, purity
क्षान्तिः आर्जवम् एव च
patience and honesty
ज्ञानं विज्ञानम् आस्तिक्यम्
knowledge, direct experience and faith
ब्रह्म-कर्म स्वभाव-जम्
are the duties of a brahmin, born of his nature
Translation

Calmness, self-control, self-discipline, purity, patience, honesty, knowledge, direct experience and faith are the duties of a brahmin, born of his nature.
