Our story

Why this site

Many of us grew up hearing the Gita, the Ramayana and temple stories from our grandparents. Our children, especially those growing up outside India, often don't get that chance. Most books and websites are either too scholarly or too short, and usually in only one language.

Sanatan Dharma Saral began as a simple wish: one place where a family can read the original Sanskrit verse, understand it in plain Hindi and English, and share it with their children. It has verse-by-verse meanings, stories, temples, festivals and kids' cards, all explained simply.

Corrections

Found a mistake? Tell us

Sanatan Dharma Saral is made by one volunteer editor, in spare time, as seva.

If you find a mistake in a verse, a meaning or a translation, please tell us. We check every correction against the original text and then fix the page.

For worship and for serious study, please learn from a qualified teacher and read a full commentary. Some are listed under Read the full texts online.

Nine languages

Read in your language

You can read the site in English, Hindi, Gujarati, Telugu, Marathi, Tamil, Bengali, Kannada and Malayalam. Each language has its own pages and its own links, so you can share a page in the language your family reads. Kannada and Malayalam are the newest and cover fewer pages so far. A page that is not yet translated opens in English.

Seva

Free, always

  • Free for everyone to use.
  • Not linked to any organisation, sampradaya, temple or political group.
  • We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages help most. It does not tell us who you are, and we never sell or share data.
  • Made with care as seva (service). If you spot a mistake, please write to us. Every correction is checked against the original texts.
