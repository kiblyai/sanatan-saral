Chapter 8 · 28 verses

The Imperishable Brahman

Every verse of chapter 8, अक्षरब्रह्मयोगः, with the Sanskrit, its pronunciation, the meaning of each word and a translation in simple English. Verses marked with a link are explained in depth on the main Gita page.

Verses 1–4: Arjuna's seven questions, and Krishna's short answers; 5–8: what a person remembers at death decides where they go, so remember Krishna always; 9–13: how the yogi leaves the body, with Om; 14–16: Krishna is easy to reach for the devoted, and for them there is no rebirth; 17–22: the day and night of Brahma, and what lies beyond them; 23–28: the two paths, of light and of smoke.

8.1 · Arjuna

Gita 8.1

अर्जुन उवाच
किं तद्ब्रह्म किमध्यात्मं किं कर्म पुरुषोत्तम ।
अधिभूतं च किं प्रोक्तमधिदैवं किमुच्यते ॥

kiṃ tadbrahma kimadhyātmaṃ kiṃ karma puruṣottama | adhibhūtaṃ ca kiṃ proktamadhidaivaṃ kimucyate ||

Word by word
किं तत् ब्रह्म
what is that Brahman
किम् अध्यात्मम्
what is the inner self
किं कर्म
what is karma
पुरुषोत्तम
O Supreme Person (Krishna)
अधिभूतं च किं प्रोक्तम्
and what is called the realm of beings
अधिदैवं किम् उच्यते
what is called the realm of the gods
Translation

Arjuna asked: Krishna, what is that Brahman? What is the inner self, and what is karma? What is meant by the realm of beings, and what by the realm of the gods?

8.2 · Arjuna

Gita 8.2

अधियज्ञः कथं कोऽत्र देहेऽस्मिन्मधुसूदन ।
प्रयाणकाले च कथं ज्ञेयोऽसि नियतात्मभिः ॥

adhiyajñaḥ kathaṃ ko'tra dehe'sminmadhusūdana | prayāṇakāle ca kathaṃ jñeyo'si niyatātmabhiḥ ||

Word by word
अधियज्ञः कथं कः अत्र
who is the lord of sacrifice here, and how
देहे अस्मिन्
in this body
मधुसूदन
O Madhusudana (Krishna)
प्रयाण-काले च
and at the time of death
कथं ज्ञेयः असि
how are you to be known
नियत-आत्मभिः
by those with self-control
Translation

Who is the lord of sacrifice in this body, and how is he here? And how are people with self-control to know you at the time of death?

8.3 · Krishna

Gita 8.3

श्री भगवानुवाच
अक्षरं ब्रह्म परमं स्वभावोऽध्यात्ममुच्यते ।
भूतभावोद्भवकरो विसर्गः कर्मसंज्ञितः ॥

akṣaraṃ brahma paramaṃ svabhāvo'dhyātmamucyate | bhūtabhāvodbhavakaro visargaḥ karmasaṃjñitaḥ ||

Word by word
अक्षरं ब्रह्म परमम्
Brahman is the supreme, which never perishes
स्वभावः अध्यात्मम् उच्यते
one's own nature is called the inner self
भूत-भाव-उद्भव-करः
which brings beings into existence
विसर्गः
the creative act
कर्म-संज्ञितः
is called karma
Translation

Krishna said: Brahman is the supreme, which never perishes. A being's own essential nature is called the inner self. The creative act that brings beings into existence is called karma.

8.4 · Krishna

Gita 8.4

अधिभूतं क्षरो भावः पुरुषश्चाधिदैवतम् ।
अधियज्ञोऽहमेवात्र देहे देहभृतां वर ॥

adhibhūtaṃ kṣaro bhāvaḥ puruṣaścādhidaivatam | adhiyajño'hamevātra dehe dehabhṛtāṃ vara ||

Word by word
अधिभूतं क्षरः भावः
the realm of beings is whatever perishes
पुरुषः च अधिदैवतम्
and the cosmic Person is the realm of the gods
अधियज्ञः अहम् एव
I myself am the lord of sacrifice
अत्र देहे
here in the body
देह-भृतां वर
O best of those who have a body (Arjuna)
Translation

The realm of beings is everything that perishes. The realm of the gods is the cosmic Person. And the lord of sacrifice, here in this body, is I myself, Arjuna.

8.5 · Krishna

Gita 8.5

अन्त-काले च

माम् एव स्मरन्

मुक्त्वा कलेवरम्

Whoever leaves the body at the time of death remembering me alone comes to my state. There is no doubt about this.

8.6 · Krishna

Gita 8.6

यं यं वापि स्मरन्भावं त्यजत्यन्ते कलेवरम् ।
तं तमेवैति कौन्तेय सदा तद्भावभावितः ॥

yaṃ yaṃ vāpi smaranbhāvaṃ tyajatyante kalevaram | taṃ tamevaiti kaunteya sadā tadbhāvabhāvitaḥ ||

Word by word
यं यं वा अपि भावम्
whatever state of being
स्मरन्
remembering
त्यजति अन्ते कलेवरम्
one leaves the body at the end
तं तम् एव एति
to that very state one goes
कौन्तेय
O son of Kunti (Arjuna)
सदा तत्-भाव-भावितः
having always been absorbed in it
Translation

Whatever a person thinks of when leaving the body at the end, Arjuna, that is what the person reaches, having been absorbed in it all along.

Gita 8.7

tasmātsarveṣu kāleṣu māmanusmara yudhya ca | mayyarpitamanobuddhirmāmevaiṣyasyasaṃśayam ||

Word by word

युध्य च

So remember me at all times, and fight. With your mind and intellect given to me, you will come to me without doubt.

8.8 · Krishna

Gita 8.8

अभ्यासयोगयुक्तेन चेतसा नान्यगामिना ।
परमं पुरुषं दिव्यं याति पार्थानुचिन्तयन् ॥

abhyāsayogayuktena cetasā nānyagāminā | paramaṃ puruṣaṃ divyaṃ yāti pārthānucintayan ||

Word by word
अभ्यास-योग-युक्तेन चेतसा
with a mind trained by steady practice
न अन्य-गामिना
that does not wander elsewhere
परमं पुरुषं दिव्यम्
the supreme, divine Person
याति
one reaches
पार्थ
O Partha (Arjuna)
अनुचिन्तयन्
by constantly thinking of him
Translation

With a mind trained by steady practice, one that does not wander elsewhere, a person who keeps thinking of the supreme divine Person reaches him, Arjuna.

8.9 · Krishna

Gita 8.9

कविं पुराणमनुशासितार
मणोरणीयांसमनुस्मरेद्यः ।
सर्वस्य धातारमचिन्त्यरूप
मादित्यवर्णं तमसः परस्तात् ॥

kaviṃ purāṇamanuśāsitāra maṇoraṇīyāṃsamanusmaredyaḥ | sarvasya dhātāramacintyarūpa mādityavarṇaṃ tamasaḥ parastāt ||

Word by word
कविं पुराणम्
the all-knowing, ancient one
अनुशासितारम्

अणोः अणीयांसम्

the supporter of all
अचिन्त्य-रूपम्

तमसः परस्तात्
beyond darkness
Translation

Think of him as all-knowing and ancient, the ruler, smaller than the smallest and the supporter of all, whose form is beyond thought, bright as the sun and beyond all darkness.

8.10 · Krishna

Gita 8.10

प्रयाणकाले मनसाऽचलेन
भक्त्या युक्तो योगबलेन चैव ।
भ्रुवोर्मध्ये प्राणमावेश्य सम्यक्
स तं परं पुरुषमुपैति दिव्यम् ॥

prayāṇakāle manasā'calena bhaktyā yukto yogabalena caiva | bhruvormadhye prāṇamāveśya samyak sa taṃ paraṃ puruṣamupaiti divyam ||

Word by word
प्रयाण-काले
at the time of death
मनसा अचलेन
with an unmoving mind
भक्त्या युक्तः
full of devotion
योग-बलेन च एव
and with the strength of yoga
भ्रुवोः मध्ये
between the eyebrows
प्राणम् आवेश्य सम्यक्
fixing the breath of life fully
सः तं परं पुरुषम् उपैति दिव्यम्
that person reaches the supreme divine Person
Translation

Whoever remembers him so at the time of death, with a steady mind, full of devotion and the strength of yoga, with the breath of life held firmly between the eyebrows, reaches that supreme divine Person.

Gita 8.11

यदक्षरं वेदविदो वदन्ति
विशन्ति यद्यतयो वीतरागाः ।

Word by word

संग्रहेण प्रवक्ष्ये

Translation

I shall tell you in brief of that goal which the knowers of the Vedas call imperishable, which seekers free from attachment enter, and for which people live a life of self-restraint.

Gita 8.21
