Chapter 11 · 55 verses

The Vision of the Universal Form

Every verse of chapter 11, विश्वरूपदर्शनयोगः, with the Sanskrit, its pronunciation, the meaning of each word and a translation in simple English. Verses marked with a link are explained in depth on the main Gita page.

Verses 1–4: Arjuna asks to see Krishna's divine form; 5–8: Krishna gives him divine sight; 9–14: Sanjaya describes the form, bright as a thousand suns; 15–31: Arjuna describes what he sees, first wonderful and then terrifying, and asks “Who are you?”; 32–34: “I am time”, and Arjuna is to be only the instrument; 35–46: Arjuna's praise, his apology, and his request to see the gentle form again; 47–55: Krishna returns to his familiar form, and says that only devotion can reach this vision.

11.1 · Arjuna

Gita 11.1

अर्जुन उवाच
मदनुग्रहाय परमं गुह्यमध्यात्मसंज्ञितम् ।
यत्त्वयोक्तं वचस्तेन मोहोऽयं विगतो मम ॥

madanugrahāya paramaṃ guhyamadhyātmasaṃjñitam | yattvayoktaṃ vacastena moho'yaṃ vigato mama ||

Word by word
मत्-अनुग्रहाय
out of kindness to me
परमं गुह्यम्
the highest secret
अध्यात्म-संज्ञितम्
about the Self
यत् त्वया उक्तं वचः
the words you have spoken
तेन मोहः अयं विगतः मम
by them this delusion of mine is gone
Translation

Arjuna said: Out of kindness to me you have told me the highest secret, about the Self. Your words have removed my delusion.

Gita 11.2

भवाप्ययौ हि भूतानां श्रुतौ विस्तरशो मया ।
त्वत्तः कमलपत्राक्ष माहात्म्यमपि चाव्ययम् ॥

bhavāpyayau hi bhūtānāṃ śrutau vistaraśo mayā | tvattaḥ kamalapatrākṣa māhātmyamapi cāvyayam ||

Word by word
भव-अप्ययौ हि भूतानाम्

श्रुतौ विस्तरशः मया

त्वत्तः
from you
कमल-पत्र-अक्ष

Translation

From you, lotus-eyed Krishna, I have heard in detail how beings come into existence and pass away, and also of your greatness, which never ends.

Krishna said: Arjuna, see my forms in their hundreds and thousands: divine, of many kinds, and of many colours and shapes.

ज्ञातुं द्रष्टुं च तत्त्वेन
be known and truly seen
प्रवेष्टुं च
and entered into
परन्तप
O scorcher of foes (Arjuna)
Translation

But by devotion given to me alone, Arjuna, I can be known in this form, truly seen, and entered into.

Gita 11.55

मत्कर्मकृन्मत्परमो मद्भक्तः सङ्गवर्जितः ।
निर्वैरः सर्वभूतेषु यः स मामेति पाण्डव ॥

matkarmakṛnmatparamo madbhaktaḥ saṅgavarjitaḥ | nirvairaḥ sarvabhūteṣu yaḥ sa māmeti pāṇḍava ||

Word by word
मत्-कर्म-कृत्
who works for me
मत्-परमः
who holds me as the highest
मत्-भक्तः
who is devoted to me
सङ्ग-वर्जितः
who is free from attachment
निर्वैरः सर्व-भूतेषु
who has no hatred for any being
यः सः माम् एति
that person comes to me
पाण्डव
O son of Pandu (Arjuna)
Translation

Whoever works for me, holds me as the highest goal, is devoted to me, is free from attachment and bears no hatred towards any being, comes to me, Arjuna.
