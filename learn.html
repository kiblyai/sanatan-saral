Sanatan Dharma is also known as Hinduism or the Hindu religion (हिन्दू धर्म). This site is a simple guide to Hinduism: its scriptures, beliefs, practices and festivals.सनातन धर्म को हिन्दू धर्म (Hinduism) भी कहा जाता है। यह साइट हिन्दू धर्म की सरल मार्गदर्शिका है: इसके ग्रन्थ, मान्यताएँ, आचरण और पर्व।

Translations are simplified for understanding. For worship and study, learn correct pronunciation from a qualified teacher and read a full commentary.
