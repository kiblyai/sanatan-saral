Chapter 9 · 34 verses

The Royal Knowledge and Royal Secret

Every verse of chapter 9, राजविद्याराजगुह्ययोगः, with the Sanskrit, its pronunciation, the meaning of each word and a translation in simple English. Verses marked with a link are explained in depth on the main Gita page.

Verses 1–3: the king of knowledge and the king of secrets; 4–10: how Krishna fills and supports the world without being bound by it; 11–15: the foolish who look down on him, and the great souls who worship him; 16–19: “I am the ritual, the father, the goal”; 20–25: heaven does not last, and each worshipper reaches what they worship; 26–28: a leaf, a flower, a fruit, and offering everything; 29–34: Krishna is the same to all, and no devotee of his is ever lost.

9.1 · Krishna

Gita 9.1

श्री भगवानुवाच
इदं तु ते गुह्यतमं प्रवक्ष्याम्यनसूयवे ।
ज्ञानं विज्ञानसहितं यज्ज्ञात्वा मोक्ष्यसेऽशुभात् ॥

idaṃ tu te guhyatamaṃ pravakṣyāmyanasūyave | jñānaṃ vijñānasahitaṃ yajjñātvā mokṣyase'śubhāt ||

Word by word
इदं तु ते गुह्यतमम्
this most secret thing, to you
प्रवक्ष्यामि
I shall tell
अनसूयवे
who do not find fault
ज्ञानं विज्ञान-सहितम्
knowledge together with direct experience
यत् ज्ञात्वा
knowing which
मोक्ष्यसे अशुभात्
you will be freed from what is harmful
Translation

Krishna said: Since you do not find fault, I shall tell you this deepest secret: knowledge together with direct experience. Knowing it, you will be freed from all that is harmful.

9.2 · Krishna

Gita 9.2

राजविद्या राजगुह्यं पवित्रमिदमुत्तमम् ।
प्रत्यक्षावगमं धर्म्यं सुसुखं कर्तुमव्ययम् ॥

rājavidyā rājaguhyaṃ pavitramidamuttamam | pratyakṣāvagamaṃ dharmyaṃ susukhaṃ kartumavyayam ||

Word by word
राज-विद्या
the king of knowledge
राज-गुह्यम्
the king of secrets
पवित्रम् इदम् उत्तमम्
this is the greatest purifier
प्रत्यक्ष-अवगमम्
understood by direct experience
धर्म्यम्
in keeping with dharma
सु-सुखं कर्तुम्
very easy to practise
अव्ययम्
and lasting
Translation

This is the king of knowledge and the king of secrets, the greatest purifier. It can be known by direct experience, it is in keeping with dharma, it is very easy to practise, and it lasts for ever.

Gita 9.4

Gita 9.5

न च मत्स्थानि भूतानि पश्य मे योगमैश्वरम् ।
भूतभृन्न च भूतस्थो ममात्मा भूतभावनः ॥

पश्य मे योगम् ऐश्वरम्

भूत-भृत्

And yet beings do not rest in me. See my divine power! My self brings all beings into existence and supports them, yet does not rest in them.

9.20 · Krishna

Gita 9.20

त्रैविद्या मां सोमपाः पूतपापा
