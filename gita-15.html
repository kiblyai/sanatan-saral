Chapter 15 · 20 verses

The Supreme Person

Every verse of chapter 15, पुरुषोत्तमयोगः, with the Sanskrit, its pronunciation, the meaning of each word and a translation in simple English. Verses marked with a link are explained in depth on the main Gita page.

Verses 1–5: the upside-down peepal tree of worldly life, and the axe of non-attachment; 6: Krishna's home, which no sun lights; 7–11: the soul, a part of Krishna, as it takes and leaves bodies; 12–15: Krishna as the light of the sun, the strength of the earth, the fire of digestion and the one seated in every heart; 16–20: the perishable, the imperishable, and the Supreme Person beyond both.

15.1 · Krishna

Gita 15.1

श्री भगवानुवाच
ऊर्ध्वमूलमधःशाखमश्वत्थं प्राहुरव्ययम् ।
छन्दांसि यस्य पर्णानि यस्तं वेद स वेदवित् ॥

ūrdhvamūlamadhaḥśākhamaśvatthaṃ prāhuravyayam | chandāṃsi yasya parṇāni yastaṃ veda sa vedavit ||

Word by word
ऊर्ध्व-मूलम्
with its roots above
अधः-शाखम्
and its branches below
अश्वत्थं प्राहुः अव्ययम्
they speak of an everlasting peepal tree
छन्दांसि यस्य पर्णानि
whose leaves are the Vedic hymns
यः तं वेद सः वेद-वित्
whoever knows it knows the Vedas
Translation

Krishna said: They speak of an everlasting peepal tree with its roots above and its branches below, whose leaves are the Vedic hymns. Whoever knows this tree knows the Vedas.

15.2 · Krishna

Gita 15.2

अधश्चोर्ध्वं प्रसृतास्तस्य शाखा
गुणप्रवृद्धा विषयप्रवालाः ।
अधश्च मूलान्यनुसन्ततानि
कर्मानुबन्धीनि मनुष्यलोके ॥

adhaścordhvaṃ prasṛtāstasya śākhā guṇapravṛddhā viṣayapravālāḥ | adhaśca mūlānyanusantatāni karmānubandhīni manuṣyaloke ||

Word by word
अधः च ऊर्ध्वं प्रसृताः तस्य शाखाः
its branches spread downward and upward
गुण-प्रवृद्धाः
fed by the gunas
विषय-प्रवालाः
with the objects of the senses as their buds
अधः च मूलानि अनुसन्ततानि
and its roots stretch downward
कर्म-अनुबन्धीनि मनुष्य-लोके
binding people to action in the human world
Translation

Its branches spread downward and upward, fed by the gunas, with the objects of the senses as their buds. Its roots also stretch downward, binding people to action in the human world.

15.3 · Krishna

Gita 15.3

न रूपमस्येह तथोपलभ्यते
नान्तो न चादिर्न च सम्प्रतिष्ठा ।
अश्वत्थमेनं सुविरूढमूल
मसङ्गशस्त्रेण दृढेन छित्त्वा ॥

na rūpamasyeha tathopalabhyate nānto na cādirna ca sampratiṣṭhā | aśvatthamenaṃ suvirūḍhamūla masaṅgaśastreṇa dṛḍhena chittvā ||

Word by word
न रूपम् अस्य इह तथा उपलभ्यते
its form is not seen here as it is
न अन्तः न च आदिः न च सम्प्रतिष्ठा
nor its end, its beginning or its foundation
अश्वत्थम् एनं सु-विरूढ-मूलम्
this peepal tree with its deep roots
असङ्ग-शस्त्रेण दृढेन छित्त्वा
having cut it down with the strong axe of non-attachment
Translation

Its real form cannot be seen here, nor its end, its beginning or its foundation. Cut down this deep-rooted tree with the strong axe of non-attachment.

15.4 · Krishna

Gita 15.4

ततः पदं तत्परिमार्गितव्यं
यस्मिन्गता न निवर्तन्ति भूयः ।
तमेव चाद्यं पुरुषं प्रपद्ये
यतः प्रवृत्तिः प्रसृता पुराणी ॥

tataḥ padaṃ tatparimārgitavyaṃ yasmingatā na nivartanti bhūyaḥ | tameva cādyaṃ puruṣaṃ prapadye yataḥ pravṛttiḥ prasṛtā purāṇī ||

Word by word
ततः पदं तत् परिमार्गितव्यम्
then that goal must be sought
यस्मिन् गताः न निवर्तन्ति भूयः
from which those who reach it never return
तम् एव च आद्यं पुरुषं प्रपद्ये
“I take refuge in that first Person”
यतः प्रवृत्तिः प्रसृता पुराणी
from whom this ancient activity has flowed
Translation

Then seek that goal from which no one returns, saying: “I take refuge in that first Person, from whom this ancient stream of activity has flowed.”

15.5 · Krishna

Gita 15.5

निर्मानमोहा जितसङ्गदोषा
अध्यात्मनित्या विनिवृत्तकामाः ।
द्वन्द्वैर्विमुक्ताः सुखदुःखसंज्ञै
र्गच्छन्त्यमूढाः पदमव्ययं तत् ॥

nirmānamohā jitasaṅgadoṣā adhyātmanityā vinivṛttakāmāḥ | dvandvairvimuktāḥ sukhaduḥkhasaṃjñai rgacchantyamūḍhāḥ padamavyayaṃ tat ||

Word by word
निर्मान-मोहाः
free from pride and delusion
जित-सङ्ग-दोषाः
having conquered the fault of attachment
अध्यात्म-नित्याः
always dwelling in the Self
विनिवृत्त-कामाः
with their desires stilled
द्वन्द्वैः विमुक्ताः सुख-दुःख-संज्ञैः
freed from the pairs known as pleasure and pain
गच्छन्ति अमूढाः पदम् अव्ययं तत्
the undeluded reach that eternal goal
Translation

Free from pride and delusion, having conquered the fault of attachment, always dwelling in the Self, with their desires stilled and freed from the pairs called pleasure and pain, the undeluded reach that eternal goal.

15.6 · Krishna

Gita 15.6

न तद्भासयते सूर्यो न शशाङ्को न पावकः ।
यद्गत्वा न निवर्तन्ते तद्धाम परमं मम ॥

na tadbhāsayate sūryo na śaśāṅko na pāvakaḥ | yadgatvā na nivartante taddhāma paramaṃ mama ||

Word by word
न तत् भासयते सूर्यः
the sun does not light it
न शशाङ्कः न पावकः
nor the moon, nor fire
यत् गत्वा न निवर्तन्ते
having gone there, they do not return
तत् धाम परमं मम
that is my supreme home
Translation

The sun does not light that place, nor the moon, nor fire. Those who go there do not return. That is my supreme home.

15.7 · Krishna

Gita 15.7

ममैवांशो जीवलोके जीवभूतः सनातनः ।
मनःषष्ठानीन्द्रियाणि प्रकृतिस्थानि कर्षति ॥

mamaivāṃśo jīvaloke jīvabhūtaḥ sanātanaḥ | manaḥṣaṣṭhānīndriyāṇi prakṛtisthāni karṣati ||

Word by word
मम एव अंशः
a part of me alone
जीव-लोके जीव-भूतः सनातनः
becomes the eternal living soul in the world of the living
मनः-षष्ठानि इन्द्रियाणि
the senses, with the mind as the sixth
प्रकृति-स्थानि कर्षति
which rest in nature, it draws to itself
Translation

An eternal part of me becomes the living soul in the world of the living. It draws to itself the five senses and the mind, the sixth, which rest in nature.

15.8 · Krishna

Gita 15.8

शरीरं यदवाप्नोति यच्चाप्युत्क्रामतीश्वरः ।
गृहीत्वैतानि संयाति वायुर्गन्धानिवाशयात् ॥

śarīraṃ yadavāpnoti yaccāpyutkrāmatīśvaraḥ | gṛhītvaitāni saṃyāti vāyurgandhānivāśayāt ||

Word by word
शरीरं यत् अवाप्नोति
when it takes a body
यत् च अपि उत्क्रामति
and when it leaves one
ईश्वरः
the lord of the body, the soul
गृहीत्वा एतानि संयाति
it takes these along and goes
वायुः गन्धान् इव आशयात्
as the wind carries scents from their source
Translation

When the soul, the lord of the body, takes a body and when it leaves one, it carries these along, as the wind carries scents from their source.

15.9 · Krishna

Gita 15.9

श्रोत्रं चक्षुः स्पर्शनं च रसनं घ्राणमेव च ।
अधिष्ठाय मनश्चायं विषयानुपसेवते ॥

śrotraṃ cakṣuḥ sparśanaṃ ca rasanaṃ ghrāṇameva ca | adhiṣṭhāya manaścāyaṃ viṣayānupasevate ||

Word by word
श्रोत्रं चक्षुः स्पर्शनं च
hearing, sight and touch
रसनं घ्राणम् एव च
taste and smell
अधिष्ठाय मनः च अयम्
presiding over these and the mind
विषयान् उपसेवते
it experiences the objects of the senses
Translation

Presiding over hearing, sight, touch, taste and smell, and also the mind, it experiences the objects of the senses.

15.10 · Krishna

Gita 15.10

उत्क्रामन्तं स्थितं वापि भुञ्जानं वा गुणान्वितम् ।
विमूढा नानुपश्यन्ति पश्यन्ति ज्ञानचक्षुषः ॥

utkrāmantaṃ sthitaṃ vāpi bhuñjānaṃ vā guṇānvitam | vimūḍhā nānupaśyanti paśyanti jñānacakṣuṣaḥ ||

Word by word
उत्क्रामन्तं स्थितं वा अपि
whether it is leaving or staying
भुञ्जानं वा गुण-अन्वितम्
or experiencing, joined with the gunas
विमूढाः न अनुपश्यन्ति
the deluded do not see it
पश्यन्ति ज्ञान-चक्षुषः
those with the eye of knowledge see
Translation

The deluded do not see it, whether it is leaving the body, staying in it, or experiencing the world through the gunas. Those who have the eye of knowledge see it.

15.11 · Krishna

Gita 15.11

यतन्तो योगिनश्चैनं पश्यन्त्यात्मन्यवस्थितम् ।
यतन्तोऽप्यकृतात्मानो नैनं पश्यन्त्यचेतसः ॥

yatanto yoginaścainaṃ paśyantyātmanyavasthitam | yatanto'pyakṛtātmāno nainaṃ paśyantyacetasaḥ ||

Word by word
यतन्तः योगिनः च एनं पश्यन्ति
yogis who strive see it
आत्मनि अवस्थितम्
living in themselves
यतन्तः अपि अकृत-आत्मानः
but those whose minds are not prepared, even if they strive
न एनं पश्यन्ति अचेतसः
do not see it, lacking understanding
Translation

Yogis who strive see it living within themselves. But those whose minds are not prepared and who lack understanding do not see it, even if they strive.

15.12 · Krishna

Gita 15.12

यदादित्यगतं तेजो जगद्भासयतेऽखिलम् ।

yadādityagataṃ tejo jagadbhāsayate'khilam | yaccandramasi yaccāgnau tattejo viddhi māmakam ||

Word by word
यत् आदित्य-गतं तेजः
the light that is in the sun
जगत् भासयते अखिलम्
and lights up the whole world
यत् चन्द्रमसि यत् च अग्नौ

तत् तेजः विद्धि मामकम्
know that light to be mine
Translation

The light of the sun that lights up the whole world, and the light in the moon and in fire: know that light to be mine.

15.13 · Krishna

Gita 15.13

गामाविश्य च भूतानि धारयाम्यहमोजसा ।
पुष्णामि चौषधीः सर्वाः सोमो भूत्वा रसात्मकः ॥

gāmāviśya ca bhūtāni dhārayāmyahamojasā | puṣṇāmi cauṣadhīḥ sarvāḥ somo bhūtvā rasātmakaḥ ||

Word by word
गाम् आविश्य च
entering the earth
भूतानि धारयामि अहम् ओजसा
I support all beings with my strength
पुष्णामि च ओषधीः सर्वाः
and I nourish all plants
सोमः भूत्वा रस-आत्मकः
becoming the moon, full of sap
Translation

Entering the earth, I support all beings with my strength. Becoming the moon, full of life-giving sap, I nourish all plants.

15.14 · Krishna

Gita 15.14

अहं वैश्वानरो भूत्वा प्राणिनां देहमाश्रितः ।
प्राणापानसमायुक्तः पचाम्यन्नं चतुर्विधम् ॥

ahaṃ vaiśvānaro bhūtvā prāṇināṃ dehamāśritaḥ | prāṇāpānasamāyuktaḥ pacāmyannaṃ caturvidham ||

Word by word
अहं वैश्वानरः भूत्वा
becoming the fire of digestion
प्राणिनां देहम् आश्रितः
living in the bodies of living beings
प्राण-अपान-समायुक्तः
joined with the breath going in and out
पचामि अन्नं चतुर्विधम्
I digest the four kinds of food
Translation

Becoming the fire of digestion, I live in the bodies of living beings. Joined with the breath that goes in and out, I digest the four kinds of food.

15.15 · Krishna

Gita 15.15

सर्वस्य चाहं हृदि सन्निविष्टो
मत्तः स्मृतिर्ज्ञानमपोहनं च ।
वेदैश्च सर्वैरहमेव वेद्यो
वेदान्तकृद्वेदविदेव चाहम् ॥

sarvasya cāhaṃ hṛdi sanniviṣṭo mattaḥ smṛtirjñānamapohanaṃ ca | vedaiśca sarvairahameva vedyo vedāntakṛdvedavideva cāham ||

Word by word
सर्वस्य च अहं हृदि सन्निविष्टः
I am seated in the heart of everyone
मत्तः स्मृतिः ज्ञानम् अपोहनं च
from me come memory, knowledge and their loss
वेदैः च सर्वैः अहम् एव वेद्यः
I alone am to be known through all the Vedas
वेदान्त-कृत्
I am the author of Vedanta
वेद-वित् एव च अहम्
and I am the knower of the Vedas
Translation

I am seated in the heart of everyone. From me come memory, knowledge and the loss of both. I alone am to be known through all the Vedas. I am the author of Vedanta, and I am the knower of the Vedas.

15.16 · Krishna

Gita 15.16

द्वाविमौ पुरुषौ लोके क्षरश्चाक्षर एव च ।
क्षरः सर्वाणि भूतानि कूटस्थोऽक्षर उच्यते ॥

dvāvimau puruṣau loke kṣaraścākṣara eva ca | kṣaraḥ sarvāṇi bhūtāni kūṭastho'kṣara ucyate ||

Word by word
द्वौ इमौ पुरुषौ लोके
there are two kinds of person in the world
क्षरः च अक्षरः एव च

Translation

There are two kinds of person in this world: the perishable and the imperishable. All beings are the perishable. The unchanging is called the imperishable.

15.18 · Krishna

Gita 15.18

यस्मात्क्षरमतीतोऽहमक्षरादपि चोत्तमः ।

15.19 · Krishna

Gita 15.19

यो मामेवमसम्मूढो जानाति पुरुषोत्तमम् ।

whoever, undeluded, knows me in this way

as the Supreme Person

Translation

Whoever, free from delusion, knows me in this way as the Supreme Person knows everything, Arjuna, and worships me with their whole being.

15.20 · Krishna

Gita 15.20

इति गुह्यतमं शास्त्रमिदमुक्तं मयाऽनघ ।
एतद्बुद्ध्वा बुद्धिमान्स्यात्कृतकृत्यश्च भारत ॥

iti guhyatamaṃ śāstramidamuktaṃ mayā'nagha | etadbuddhvā buddhimānsyātkṛtakṛtyaśca bhārata ||

Word by word
इति गुह्यतमं शास्त्रम् इदम् उक्तं मया
so I have taught this most secret teaching
अनघ
O sinless one (Arjuna)
एतत् बुद्ध्वा
understanding this
बुद्धिमान् स्यात्
a person becomes wise
कृत-कृत्यः च
and has done all there is to do
भारत
O descendant of Bharata
Translation

So I have taught you this most secret teaching, Arjuna. Whoever understands it becomes wise and has done all that there is to do.
